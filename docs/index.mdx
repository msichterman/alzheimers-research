Welcome to **Alzheimer's Research** — a comprehensive, living knowledge base dedicated to Alzheimer's disease science, clinical trials, biomarkers, and patient care. 

Whether you are a **patient or family member** seeking compassionate and plain-language clarity, a **researcher or clinician** analyzing trial data and paper reviews, or an **engineer** connecting an AI agent directly to the corpus, this platform is structured to give you transparent, evidence-grounded answers.

## Key Portals & Core Topics

<Tabs param="portal">
  <Tab title="For Patients & Families">
    ### Navigating Your Journey
    We translate cutting-edge medical discoveries into actionable, compassionate guidance:

    * **[Next Steps After Diagnosis](/journey/newly-diagnosed):** Practical legal, financial, medical, and emotional checklist.
    * **[Diagnostic Tests & Biomarkers](/journey/diagnostic-tests-biomarkers):** Memory assessments, blood tests (p-tau217), brain MRIs, and PET scans.
    * **[Treatments & Medications](/journey/treatments-and-medications):** Disease-modifying antibodies (Leqembi, Kisunla), symptomatic options, and ARIA safety.
    * **[Understanding Amyloid Plaques](/journey/amyloid-hypothesis-consumer):** Plain-language breakdown of brain plaques and therapeutic targets.
    * **[Caregiver Support & Daily Living](/journey/caregiver-guide):** Communication techniques, safety adaptations, and preventing burnout.
    * **[Lifestyle & Brain Health](/journey/lifestyle-and-brain-health):** Evidence-based multidomain interventions (FINGER, MIND diet, cardiovascular care).
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="For Researchers & Clinicians">
    ### Evidence-Grounded Scientific Syntheses
    Every paper note and trial record includes critical appraisal, funding disclosures, and methodological assessments:

    * **[The Amyloid Hypothesis Debate](/research/amyloid-hypothesis):** Multi-decade analysis of genetic evidence, therapeutic triumphs, and lingering controversies.
    * **[Biomarker Landscape](/research/biomarkers):** Liquid biopsy developments (plasma p-tau217, p-tau181, GFAP, NfL) and PET imaging standardization.
    * **[Paper Review Archive](/research/papers):** Over 100+ analyzed publications with claim-by-claim verification and bias tracking.
    * **[Key Authors & Investigators](/research/authors):** Directory of leading researchers in neuropathology, genetics, and clinical trial design.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="For AI Agents & Developers">
    ### Native Machine-Readable Surfaces
    This knowledge base is fully AI-native. Every page is exposed to assistants, IDEs, and autonomous pipelines without web scraping:

    * **MCP Server (`/mcp`):** Connect via Model Context Protocol for typed tools (`search_docs`, `get_page`, `list_pages`, `get_navigation`).
    * **Markdown Twins:** Append `.md` to any URL for clean Markdown, or `.mdx` for source.
    * **LLM Ingestion Index:** [`/llms.txt`](/llms.txt) provides a full table of contents; [`/llms-full.txt`](/llms-full.txt) provides the entire corpus in a single fetch.
    * **Readability Manifest:** [`/agent-readability.json`](/agent-readability.json) advertises all endpoints and tool capabilities.
    * Learn more in our [For Agents Guide](/agents) and [Machine Surfaces](/agents/surfaces).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Content Ingestion & Pipeline

This repository is continuously maintained and enriched with our automated discovery, validation, and publishing pipeline:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Literature & Trial Discovery">
    Automated ingestion monitors PubMed, Europe PMC, and ClinicalTrials.gov for newly released Alzheimer's publications and registry updates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Critical Extraction & Review">
    Research agents perform deep claim extraction, bias identification, trial design cross-examination, and funding conflict auditing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bilingual Publishing">
    Results are compiled into structured, peer-reviewed Markdown pages alongside plain-language family summaries and interactive timeline updates.
  </Step>
</Steps>
