## Foundations

<Steps>
  <Step title="1906 — Alzheimer describes the disease">
    Alois Alzheimer reports plaques and tangles in the brain of Auguste Deter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="1984 — Amyloid-beta identified">
    Glenner and Wong isolate and sequence the amyloid-beta peptide from
    cerebrovascular amyloid. Foundation of the
    [amyloid hypothesis](/research/amyloid-hypothesis).
  </Step>
  <Step title="1993 — APOE4 linked to risk">
    The APOE ε4 allele is identified as the major genetic risk factor for
    late-onset Alzheimer's.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2013 — NLRP3 inflammasome linked to Alzheimer's pathology">
    A foundational mouse study demonstrates that knocking out the NLRP3 inflammasome protects against memory loss and amyloid-beta deposition, sparking intense interest in neuroinflammation targets. [Read the paper](/research/papers/nlrp3-alzheimers-heneka-2013).
  </Step>
  <Step title="2013 — TREM2 variants linked to increased Alzheimer's risk">
    A landmark [study](/research/papers/trem2-variants-guerreiro-2013) identifies the TREM2 R47H variant as a major genetic risk factor, cementing the role of microglial inflammation in Alzheimer's disease.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2013 — TREM2 R47H variant linked to Alzheimer's risk">
    A genetic sequencing study of Icelanders discovers that a rare missense mutation in the TREM2 gene triples the risk of late-onset Alzheimer's disease, highlighting the role of microglial inflammation in the disease process ([Jonsson et al.](/research/papers/trem2-r47h-alzheimers-risk)).
  </Step>
  <Step title="2017-06 — Discovery of Disease-Associated Microglia (DAM)">
    Researchers map a novel, TREM2-dependent microglial state that clusters around amyloid plaques, sparking a wave of biotech investment into TREM2 agonists. Detailed in [Keren-Shaul et al.](/research/papers/disease-associated-microglia-cell-2017).
  </Step>
  <Step title="2019-05 — First major single-cell transcriptomic map of the Alzheimer's brain published">
    A landmark [study of 80,660 single-nucleus transcriptomes](/research/papers/mathys-single-cell-transcriptomics-2019) from the human prefrontal cortex revealed profound cell-type-specific and sex-specific transcriptional responses to Alzheimer's pathology.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2022-11 — Large-scale exome sequencing links ATP8B4 and ABCA1 to Alzheimer's risk">
    An analysis of over 32,000 exomes identified rare, damaging variants in lipid metabolism genes *ATP8B4* and *ABCA1* as significant risk factors, reinforcing the role of microglial function in the disease ([Holstege et al.](/research/papers/atp8b4-abca1-exome-sequencing-ad)).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Disease-modifying era

<Steps>
  <Step title="2012-08 — Intravenous bapineuzumab program terminated">
    Pfizer and Janssen discontinue all global Phase 3 trials of the anti-amyloid antibody bapineuzumab after [North American trials](/research/papers/bapineuzumab-phase-3-mild-moderate) fail to show clinical benefit in mild-to-moderate Alzheimer's disease.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2012-10 — PRIME trial begins">
    Biogen initiates the Phase 1b [PRIME study](/research/papers/aducanumab-prime-registry) to evaluate the safety and amyloid-clearing efficacy of multiple aducanumab doses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2012-12 — Lecanemab Phase 2b trial begins">
    The dose-finding Phase 2b study (Study 201) for lecanemab enrolls its first participants to evaluate amyloid clearance and clinical efficacy. ([Lecanemab Phase 2b](/research/papers/lecanemab-phase-2b))
  </Step>
  <Step title="2015-06 — FINGER trial demonstrates benefits of multidomain lifestyle intervention">
    The [FINGER trial](/trials/finger-nct01041989) reads out 2-year results showing modest cognitive performance improvements, providing interventional support for multidomain lifestyle modifications.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2017-02 — EPOCH trial of verubecestat terminated for futility">
    The Phase 2/3 trial evaluating verubecestat in mild-to-moderate Alzheimer's disease was terminated early, marking the first major failure in Merck's BACE-1 inhibitor clinical program ([EPOCH](/research/papers/verubecestat-epoch)).
  </Step>
  <Step title="2017-02 — Verubecestat EPOCH trial terminated for futility">
    Merck halts its Phase 2/3 EPOCH trial of the BACE-1 inhibitor verubecestat in mild-to-moderate Alzheimer's after an independent committee finds no chance of clinical benefit, marking a major setback for the BACE inhibitor class. [Read the EPOCH results](/research/papers/verubecestat-epoch).
  </Step>
  <Step title="2017-04 — Verubecestat EPOCH trial terminated">
    Merck halts the Phase 2/3 EPOCH trial of its BACE inhibitor verubecestat in mild-to-moderate Alzheimer's disease due to futility and safety signals, including worsened brain atrophy, detailed in the [EPOCH registry results](/research/papers/verubecestat-epoch).
  </Step>
  <Step title="2018 — NIA-AA proposes biological AT(N) definition of Alzheimer's">
    The [2018 NIA-AA Research Framework](/research/papers/nia-aa-research-framework-2018) radically shifted the field by defining Alzheimer's disease through biomarkers (amyloid, tau, neurodegeneration) rather than clinical symptoms, paving the way for targeted clinical trials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2018-02 — APECS trial of verubecestat terminated for futility">
    Merck halted its Phase 3 trial of the BACE-1 inhibitor verubecestat in prodromal Alzheimer's after interim data showed the drug worsened cognitive outcomes despite successfully clearing amyloid, dealing a significant blow to the class ([APECS](/research/papers/verubecestat-apecs)).
  </Step>
  <Step title="2018-06 — Lanabecestat Phase 3 program halted for futility">
    AstraZeneca and Eli Lilly terminated the AMARANTH and DAYBREAK-ALZ trials of their BACE1 inhibitor lanabecestat after an interim analysis revealed it [failed to slow cognitive decline](/research/papers/lanabecestat-amaranth-daybreak-alz) despite effectively lowering amyloid levels.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2019-02 — SPRINT MIND shows intensive blood pressure control reduces MCI">
    The SPRINT MIND trial demonstrates that intensive antihypertensive therapy reduces the incidence of mild cognitive impairment, validating vascular health as a key target for [dementia prevention](/research/papers/lancet-commission-2020-dementia-prevention).
  </Step>
  <Step title="2019-03 — Aducanumab Phase 3 trials halted for futility">
    Biogen halts the [ENGAGE](/trials/engage-nct02477800) and [EMERGE](/trials/emerge-nct02484547) trials after an independent data monitoring committee finds they are unlikely to meet their primary endpoints.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2019-03 — Phase 3 aducanumab trials halted for futility">
    Biogen terminates the [EMERGE](/trials/emerge-nct02484547) and [ENGAGE](/trials/engage-nct02477800) trials based on an independent interim analysis, though later post-hoc data mining led to a highly controversial FDA approval.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2019-03 — Clarity AD Phase 3 trial begins">
    Eisai and Biogen launch the pivotal Phase 3 [Clarity AD](/trials/lecanemab-clarity-ad) trial to evaluate lecanemab's efficacy in early Alzheimer's disease.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2019-03 — CLARITY-AD trial begins">
    Eisai initiates the pivotal Phase 3 CLARITY-AD trial to evaluate lecanemab in early Alzheimer's disease. [FDA Approval Package](/research/papers/lecanemab-fda-approval-package)
  </Step>
  <Step title="2019-03 — Clarity AD trial begins">
    Eisai initiates the pivotal Phase 3 Clarity AD trial to confirm lecanemab's efficacy in early Alzheimer's disease. ([Clarity AD](/trials/lecanemab-clarity-ad-nct03887455))
  </Step>
  <Step title="2019-08 — Aducanumab Phase 3 trials terminated">
    Biogen officially terminates the identically designed EMERGE and ENGAGE trials for futility, though they would later extract a highly controversial positive signal from [EMERGE](/research/papers/aducanumab-emerge-phase-3) to pursue FDA approval.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2019-08 — EMERGE and ENGAGE trials terminated">
    Biogen terminates the identical Phase 3 EMERGE and ENGAGE trials of aducanumab based on a futility analysis, though [post-hoc EMERGE results](/research/papers/aducanumab-emerge-registry-results) later showed conflicting signals of efficacy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2019-11 — Microglial NLRP3 inflammasome linked to tau pathology">
    A foundational [Nature study](/research/papers/ising-nlrp3-inflammasome-tau-2019) demonstrates that the microglial NLRP3 inflammasome acts as a critical biological bridge between amyloid-beta accumulation and tau pathology in mice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2020-05 — Plasma p-tau181 validated as highly accurate Alzheimer's blood test">
    A landmark study led by the University of Gothenburg demonstrates that a simple blood test for p-tau181 accurately distinguishes Alzheimer's disease from other neurodegenerative disorders, paving the way for scalable diagnostics. [Read the paper](/research/papers/plasma-ptau181-karikari-2020)
  </Step>
  <Step title="2020-07 — AHEAD 3-45 begins">
    First phase 3 prevention trial of lecanemab in preclinical AD starts
    enrolling. See [AHEAD 3-45](/trials/ahead-3-45).
  </Step>
  <Step title="2020-07 — Lancet Commission updates modifiable risk factor model">
    The Lancet Commission publishes an updated life-course model adding air pollution, traumatic brain injury, and excessive alcohol, estimating 40% of dementias are tied to 12 [modifiable risk factors](/research/papers/lancet-commission-2020-dementia-prevention).
  </Step>
  <Step title="2021-03 — Donanemab Phase 2 results published">
    The [TRAILBLAZER-ALZ](/trials/trailblazer-alz-nct03367403) trial establishes clinical proof-of-concept for donanemab's ability to clear amyloid and modestly slow cognitive decline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2021-03 — Brainshuttle AD Phase 1b/2a trial begins">
    Roche initiates dosing in [Brainshuttle AD](/trials/brainshuttle-ad-nct04639050) to evaluate trontinemab, a next-generation anti-amyloid antibody utilizing a blood-brain barrier shuttle.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2021-04 — Lecanemab Phase 2b results published">
    A [Phase 2b trial of lecanemab](/research/papers/lecanemab-phase-2b) missed its 12-month primary endpoint but showed robust amyloid clearance and signals of clinical benefit at 18 months, enabling its Phase 3 program.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2021-06 — Aducanumab accelerated approval">
    FDA grants accelerated approval to aducanumab (Aduhelm) on amyloid
    lowering; the decision is widely contested and the drug is later
    discontinued (2024).
  </Step>
  <Step title="2021-06 — ADAMANT Phase 2 trial publishes results">
    Results from the [ADAMANT Phase 2 trial](/research/papers/aadvac1-adamant-phase-2) show the active tau vaccine AADvac1 is safe but fails to significantly slow cognitive decline in a broad mild Alzheimer's population.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2021-06 — Gosuranemab program terminated">
    Following the definitive failure of the Phase 2 [TANGO trial](/research/papers/gosuranemab-tango) to slow cognitive decline, Biogen officially terminates development of the anti-tau antibody gosuranemab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2021-06 — DIAN-TU-001 misses primary endpoint">
    The DIAN-TU-001 trial reports that gantenerumab and solanezumab failed to slow cognitive decline in patients with autosomal dominant Alzheimer's disease, despite target engagement. [Read more](/trials/dian-tu-001)
  </Step>
  <Step title="2021-08 — TRAILBLAZER-ALZ 3 begins">
    First large prevention trial to enroll by blood biomarker (plasma
    p-tau217). See [TRAILBLAZER-ALZ 3](/trials/trailblazer-alz-3).
  </Step>
  <Step title="2022-03 — API ADAD Colombia trial misses primary endpoint">
    The prevention trial testing crenezumab in the PSEN1-E280A Colombian kindred [failed to demonstrate clinical benefit](/trials/api-adad-colombia-crenezumab) or significantly slow cognitive decline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2022-11 — Gantenerumab Phase 3 GRADUATE trials fail">
    Roche announces that its Phase 3 GRADUATE I and II trials failed to meet their primary clinical endpoints, as gantenerumab effectively cleared amyloid but did not significantly slow cognitive decline. [Read more](/research/papers/gantenerumab-graduate)
  </Step>
  <Step title="2022-11 — Gantenerumab Phase 3 program terminated">
    Roche discontinues development of gantenerumab after the [GRADUATE I](/research/papers/gantenerumab-graduate-1-registry) and II trials show that robust brain amyloid clearance failed to significantly slow clinical decline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2022-11 — Tau PET neocortical spread linked to 40-fold dementia risk">
    A major multi-cohort study of 1,325 cognitively unimpaired individuals ([Ossenkoppele et al.](/research/papers/amyloid-tau-pet-cognitive-decline-ossenkoppele)) demonstrates that combined amyloid and neocortical tau PET positivity strongly predicts short-term progression to dementia, validating biological definitions of preclinical Alzheimer's.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2023-01 — Clarity AD trial demonstrates efficacy">
    Primary results from the [Clarity AD](/trials/lecanemab-clarity-ad) trial show that lecanemab slowed clinical decline by 27% over 18 months, setting the stage for FDA approval.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2023-01 — FDA issues Complete Response Letter for donanemab">
    The FDA initially declined accelerated approval for donanemab due to an insufficient safety database, requesting more data to characterize long-term risks before its eventual 2024 approval. ([FDA Risk Review](/research/papers/fda-risk-review-donanemab-kisunla))
  </Step>
  <Step title="2023-01 — Clarity AD (lecanemab) Phase 3 results published">
    The pivotal Phase 3 trial demonstrated a 27% slowing of clinical decline, confirming lecanemab's efficacy and ultimately leading to full FDA approval, while highlighting severe ARIA risks in ApoE4 carriers. Supported by [Clarity AD](/trials/clarity-ad-nct03887455).
  </Step>
  <Step title="2023-01 — CLARITY AD Phase 3 results published">
    Results from the pivotal Phase 3 trial of [lecanemab](/research/papers/leqembi-fda-label) are published, demonstrating a 27% slowing of clinical decline and forming the basis for full FDA approval.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2023-01 — CLARITY-AD results published">
    The 18-month clinical outcomes from the Phase 3 CLARITY-AD trial are published, demonstrating a 27% slowing of clinical decline alongside significant ARIA risks. [FDA Approval Package](/research/papers/lecanemab-fda-approval-package)
  </Step>
  <Step title="2023-01 — FDA grants accelerated approval to lecanemab">
    The FDA granted accelerated approval to lecanemab based on amyloid plaque clearance observed in the Phase 2b Study 201, despite the trial missing its primary clinical efficacy threshold ([FDA Summary Review](/research/papers/lecanemab-study-201-fda-review)).
  </Step>
  <Step title="2023-01 — Clarity AD Phase 3 results published">
    Results from the 18-month core study demonstrate a 27% slowing of clinical decline, validating the anti-amyloid mechanism. See [Clarity AD registry](/research/papers/lecanemab-clarity-ad-registry).
  </Step>
  <Step title="2023-01 — Lecanemab receives accelerated FDA approval">
    The FDA granted accelerated approval to lecanemab (Leqembi) based on its ability to rapidly clear brain amyloid, prior to its full approval in July 2023.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2023-02 — Masitinib Phase 3 trial reports mixed results">
    AB Science published results from a Phase 3 trial of [masitinib](/research/papers/masitinib-phase-3-ab09004), showing cognitive benefit at a lower dose but failing at a higher dose amid methodological controversies.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2023-04 — Donanemab TRAILBLAZER-ALZ 2 trial reads out">
    The pivotal Phase 3 [TRAILBLAZER-ALZ 2](/research/papers/donanemab-trailblazer-alz-2) trial reaches its primary completion, demonstrating that donanemab significantly clears amyloid plaques and slows clinical decline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2023-07 — Lecanemab full FDA approval">
    Leqembi receives traditional approval after CLARITY AD shows ~27% slowing
    of decline over 18 months in early symptomatic AD.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2023-07 — TRAILBLAZER-ALZ 2 Phase 3 results published">
    Donanemab demonstrated significant slowing of clinical decline alongside notable ARIA risks in the [TRAILBLAZER-ALZ 2 Phase 3 results](/research/papers/donanemab-trailblazer-alz-2).
  </Step>
  <Step title="2023-07 — Solanezumab fails A4 trial in preclinical Alzheimer's">
    Results published from the A4 study definitively demonstrated that targeting monomeric amyloid with solanezumab does not slow cognitive decline, ending the drug's clinical development. Details in [Trial of Solanezumab in Preclinical Alzheimer’s Disease](/research/papers/solanezumab-a4-prevention).
  </Step>
  <Step title="2023-07 — Donanemab Phase 3 results published">
    Full results from the pivotal [TRAILBLAZER-ALZ 2](/research/papers/donanemab-trailblazer-alz-2) trial demonstrate donanemab slows clinical decline while robustly clearing amyloid plaques.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2024-01 — Biogen discontinues Aduhelm">
    Facing severe commercial failure, widespread skepticism over its [Phase 3 evidence](/research/papers/aducanumab-emerge-phase-3), and refusal of broad Medicare coverage, Biogen formally abandons the aducanumab program.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2024-01 — Biogen abandons aducanumab">
    Following restrictive Medicare coverage, severe commercial failure, and an unfavorable [ARIA safety profile](/research/papers/aria-aducanumab-emerge-engage), Biogen formally discontinues the aducanumab program.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2024-02 — Distinct transcriptomic profile of PSEN1-E280A published">
    Single-nucleus RNA sequencing [revealed](/research/papers/psen1-apoe3ch-transcriptomics-2024) that the PSEN1-E280A mutation triggers unique chaperone and autophagy gene signatures distinct from sporadic Alzheimer's.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2024-02 — Biogen discontinues aducanumab">
    Following severe commercial failure, safety concerns, and lack of definitive efficacy in trials like [ENGAGE](/trials/aducanumab-engage-nct02477800), Biogen permanently abandons the aducanumab franchise and terminates its confirmatory studies.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2024-03 — Canakinumab Phase 2 trial terminated">
    Novartis posts results for the early-terminated [Phase 2 trial](/trials/canakinumab-nct04795466) of the IL-1β inhibitor canakinumab, showing no cognitive benefit in early Alzheimer's and casting doubt on translating the NLRP3/IL-1β hypothesis to humans.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2024-06 — Alzheimer's Association revises diagnostic criteria">
    The Alzheimer's Association officially updates its guidelines to define Alzheimer's disease purely by biological biomarkers rather than clinical symptoms, publishing the [revised diagnostic and staging criteria](/research/papers/aa-revised-diagnostic-criteria-2024).
  </Step>
  <Step title="2024-07 — Donanemab approval">
    Kisunla approved after TRAILBLAZER-ALZ 2 shows ~35% slowing in the
    low-medium tau population.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2024-08 — First clinical evidence for APOE gene therapy">
    Interim Phase 1/2 results from the [LX1001-01](/trials/lx1001-01-nct03634007) trial show that delivering the protective APOE2 gene to APOE4 homozygotes results in target engagement and preliminary tau biomarker reductions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2024-08 — First TREM2 agonist fails in Phase 2">
    The leading TREM2-activating antibody, [AL002](/trials/al002-invoke-2-nct04592874), fails to slow cognitive decline and induces ARIA in the INVOKE-2 trial, casting doubt on the microglial activation hypothesis.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2024-08 — Aducanumab program discontinued">
    Biogen terminates the confirmatory [ENVISION](/trials/envision-nct05310071) trial and permanently discontinues the aducanumab program following overwhelming commercial and clinical controversy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2024-08 — AL002 Phase 2 trial (INVOKE-2) completes with negative results">
    Alector's TREM2-targeted antibody AL002 failed to meet its primary and secondary endpoints in early Alzheimer's disease while significantly increasing ARIA rates, severely denting the microglial hypothesis. [Review the trial data](/research/papers/al002-invoke-2-phase-2)
  </Step>
  <Step title="2024-08 — AL002 (TREM2 agonist) Phase 2 trial fails">
    Alector's TREM2 agonistic antibody AL002 fails its Phase 2 [INVOKE-2](/trials/invoke-2-al002) trial, missing primary clinical endpoints and casting doubt on antibody-based TREM2 modulation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2024-09 — Etalanetug and lecanemab combination Phase 2 begins">
    Eisai initiates a Phase 2 trial ([NCT06602258](/research/papers/etalanetug-lecanemab-phase-2-registry)) evaluating the anti-tau antibody etalanetug concurrently with the anti-amyloid lecanemab in early Alzheimer's disease.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2024-09 — TREM2 agonist AL002 fails Phase 2 INVOKE-2 trial">
    Despite strong genetic validation for TREM2 as an Alzheimer's risk factor, Alector's TREM2-stimulating antibody AL002 failed to slow clinical decline in [INVOKE-2](/trials/invoke-2-nct04592874), highlighting the difficulty of translating genetic targets into therapeutics.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2024-09 — AL002 (TREM2 agonist) fails Phase 2 trial">
    Alector's [INVOKE-2](/trials/al002-invoke-2-nct04592874) trial fails to slow cognitive decline in early Alzheimer's, challenging the therapeutic viability of activating TREM2 after symptom onset.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2024-10 — TRAILRUNNER-ALZ 3 trial begins">
    Eli Lilly launches a Phase 3 trial evaluating subcutaneous remternetug in 1,400 participants with early Alzheimer's disease to measure time to clinical progression over 255 weeks. [Read more](/research/papers/remternetug-trailrunner-alz-3)
  </Step>
  <Step title="2025-05 — First blood test cleared">
    FDA clears the Fujirebio Lumipulse plasma pTau217/β-amyloid 1-42 ratio —
    the first blood-based diagnostic for Alzheimer's. See
    [Biomarkers](/research/biomarkers).
  </Step>
  <Step title="2025-07 — EMA CHMP recommends Kisunla (donanemab) approval for APOE4 non-homozygotes">
    Following an initial rejection, the [EMA CHMP recommended approval](/research/papers/donanemab-ema-assessment-2025) for donanemab, notably restricting the indication exclusively to APOE4 heterozygotes and non-carriers to manage severe ARIA risks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2025-08 — U.S. POINTER lifestyle trial reads out">
    The U.S. POINTER study demonstrated that a structured multidomain lifestyle intervention significantly, though modestly, protected [global cognition](/research/papers/us-pointer-lifestyle-intervention) in at-risk older adults over two years.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2025-09 — Trontinemab TRONTIER 1 Phase 3 trial begins">
    Roche initiates the first of two pivotal Phase 3 trials for its TfR1-mediated 'brain shuttle' anti-amyloid antibody, trontinemab, following promising Phase 1/2 biomarker data demonstrating rapid plaque clearance and low ARIA rates. [Read more](/research/papers/trontinemab-trontier-1)
  </Step>
  <Step title="2025-11 — Phase 3 EVOKE trials for semaglutide fail">
    The Phase 3 EVOKE and EVOKE Plus programs report missing their primary endpoints, failing to show that oral semaglutide slows cognitive decline in early Alzheimer's disease. [Read more](/research/papers/evoke-plus-semaglutide-phase-3)
  </Step>
  <Step title="2025-11 — TRONTIER 2 begins">
    Roche initiates [TRONTIER 2](/trials/trontinemab-nct07170150), a massive Phase 3 program evaluating the transferrin receptor-binding Brainshuttle antibody trontinemab in early Alzheimer's disease.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2026-01 — FDA approves subcutaneous Leqembi autoinjector">
    The FDA updated the [Leqembi prescribing information](/research/papers/leqembi-fda-label-2026) to include a weekly subcutaneous maintenance injection, offering a more convenient home-administered alternative to bi-weekly IV infusions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2026-01 — INFRONT-3 trial (latozinemab) terminated">
    The Phase 3 [INFRONT-3](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04374136) trial of latozinemab, targeting the *GRN* pathway in frontotemporal dementia, was terminated after failing to meet its primary efficacy endpoint.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2026-03 — EVOKE Phase 3 trial of semaglutide fails">
    Novo Nordisk's oral semaglutide failed to slow cognitive decline in early Alzheimer's disease, leading to the discontinuation of the program. [Read the EVOKE Phase 3 analysis](/research/papers/evoke-semaglutide-phase-3).
  </Step>
  <Step title="2026-04 — Buntanetap Phase 2/3 misses primary endpoint, biomarker subgroup shows signal">
    An oral APP/Tau translation inhibitor failed its co-primary cognitive endpoints in the full trial population; a post-hoc, unvalidated biomarker-positive subgroup showed improvement, motivating a confirmatory Phase 3 — see [Buntanetap in mild-to-moderate AD](/research/papers/buntanetap-phase-2-3-ad).
  </Step>
  <Step title="2026-08 — Sanofi acquires Vigil Neuroscience">
    Sanofi acquires Vigil Neuroscience and its Phase 1 oral small-molecule TREM2 agonist VG-3927, pivoting industry focus toward small molecules for microglial targets.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Infection hypothesis

<Steps>
  <Step title="2019-03 — GAIN trial begins (COR388 in mild-to-moderate AD)">
    Pivotal Phase 2/3 test of the P. gingivalis/gingipain hypothesis reviewed in [this paper](/research/papers/p-gingivalis-cor388-alzheimers); see the [GAIN trial page](/trials/cor388-nct03823404).
  </Step>
  <Step title="2021-10 — GAIN trial fails co-primary endpoints">
    COR388 showed no significant benefit on ADAS-Cog11 or ADCS-ADL, undercutting the treatment-efficacy case made in [this review](/research/papers/p-gingivalis-cor388-alzheimers).
  </Step>
  <Step title="2022-01 — FDA holds COR388 trial over liver toxicity">
    Dose-dependent liver enzyme elevations prompted a full clinical hold on atuzaginstat; detail on the [GAIN trial page](/trials/cor388-nct03823404).
  </Step>
  <Step title="2022-08 — Cortexyme discontinues gingipain-inhibitor program">
    Sponsor abandoned COR388/atuzaginstat and rebranded as Quince Therapeutics, ending the clinical test of the P. gingivalis hypothesis reviewed [here](/research/papers/p-gingivalis-cor388-alzheimers).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Diagnostics

<Steps>
  <Step title="2021-10 — Plasma GFAP validated as early Alzheimer's biomarker">
    A pivotal multi-cohort study demonstrates that plasma GFAP significantly outperforms CSF GFAP in detecting early amyloid-β pathology, establishing momentum for accessible [blood-based diagnostics](/research/papers/plasma-gfap-benedet-2021).
  </Step>
  <Step title="2022-08 — Alzheimer's Association issues first blood biomarker guidelines">
    The Alzheimer's Association publishes [appropriate use recommendations](/research/papers/alz-association-blood-biomarkers-guidelines-2022) for blood-based biomarkers, endorsing their use in trial pre-screening and specialized clinics while strictly advising against primary care use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2024-07 — BioFINDER-Primary Care validates p-tau217">
    Interim results from the [BioFINDER - Primary Care](/trials/biofinder-pc-nct06120361) trial demonstrate that plasma p-tau217 has ~90% diagnostic accuracy in real-world primary care settings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Anti-amyloid monoclonal antibodies

<Steps>
  <Step title="2019-08 — Aducanumab Phase 3 trials terminated for futility">
    Biogen halts the ENGAGE and EMERGE trials following an interim analysis showing the drug was unlikely to meet its primary efficacy endpoints, though later post-hoc analyses of EMERGE ultimately led to an FDA approval. ([ENGAGE results](/research/papers/aducanumab-engage-phase-3))

## Biomarkers & Diagnostics

<Steps>
  <Step title="2015-01 — Centiloid Project standardizes amyloid PET">
    The [Centiloid Project](/research/papers/centiloid-project-standardization) establishes a universal 0-100 scale for quantifying amyloid plaques, enabling direct comparisons across different imaging tracers and analysis methods.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2017-07 — Plasma Aβ42/Aβ40 ratio validated for detecting CNS amyloidosis">
    Ovod et al. publish foundational data demonstrating that precise mass-spectrometry measurements of the plasma Aβ42/Aβ40 ratio strongly correlate with brain amyloid plaques, paving the way for highly accurate commercial blood tests.   </Step>
  <Step title="2019-08 — Plasma Aβ42/40 predicts brain amyloidosis">
    A high-precision mass-spectrometry blood test is shown to accurately predict amyloid PET status and future conversion in cognitively normal individuals, supported by [Schindler et al. 2019](/research/papers/plasma-abeta42-40-schindler-2019).
  </Step>
  <Step title="2020-11 — Plasma p-tau217 tracks Alzheimer's progression">
    A [longitudinal analysis](/research/papers/ptau217-longitudinal-biofinder-2020) of the BioFINDER cohort demonstrates that plasma p-tau217 levels increase over time in early Alzheimer's disease, providing first-evidence validation of the assay as a minimally invasive tool for tracking disease progression.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2024-07 — APS2 blood test demonstrates high diagnostic accuracy in primary care">
    A prospective study published in JAMA showed the APS2 blood test accurately detected Alzheimer's pathology in about 90% of symptomatic patients, significantly outperforming standard clinical evaluations by both primary care doctors and specialists. ([Blood Biomarkers to Detect Alzheimer Disease in Primary and Secondary Care](/research/papers/aps2-blood-biomarkers-primary-care-jama-2024))
  </Step>
</Steps>
