Topic pages consolidate what multiple papers say about one question; paper notes in the [Papers](/research/papers) subsection capture a single source each, dated and sorted newest first.

## Papers

One note per paper, produced by the research pipeline (or by hand from `templates/paper-note.mdx`). Each carries the publication date, source links, key findings, and a **Critical review** block — funding & conflicts, bias flags, claim strength, and cross-examination. Browse the [date-sorted index](/research/papers).

## Conventions

- **File name**: kebab-case, descriptive — `lecanemab-clarity-ad.mdx`, not `paper-1.mdx`. Papers live in `docs/research/papers/`.
- **Frontmatter**: every note sets `title`, `description`, `date` (publication date), and `search.tags` so it is filterable and sortable.
- **Sources**: link the DOI or PubMed record at the top of the note. Claims in the note should trace to a source.
- **Timeline**: if the paper marks a milestone (first evidence, approval, major readout), add it to [the timeline](/timeline) too.
