Biomarkers define Alzheimer's disease biologically (the A/T/N framework: amyloid, tau, neurodegeneration) and increasingly gate trial enrollment and treatment eligibility.

## Established

- **Amyloid PET** — gold standard for plaque burden; used as an endpoint in anti-amyloid trials.
- **CSF Aβ42/40 and p-tau** — invasive but well validated.
- **Tau PET** — tracks tangle spread; stages disease better than amyloid PET.

## Blood-based

Plasma p-tau217 is the leading blood marker; it discriminates Alzheimer's pathology with performance approaching CSF and PET. In May 2025 the FDA cleared the first blood test for Alzheimer's diagnosis (Fujirebio Lumipulse plasma pTau217/β-amyloid 1-42 ratio), which lowers the barrier to screening and trial recruitment at scale.

## Why it matters for trials

Cheap, scalable screening changes trial economics: prevention trials like [AHEAD 3-45](/trials/ahead-3-45) can pre-screen with plasma markers before confirmatory PET, cutting screen-failure costs.
