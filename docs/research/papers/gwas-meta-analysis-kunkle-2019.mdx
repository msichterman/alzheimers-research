## Takeaway

This large-scale genome-wide association meta-analysis of 94,437 individuals confirmed 20 known risk loci and identified five new ones (*IQCK*, *ACE*, *ADAM10*, *ADAMTS1*, and *WWOX*). The genetic evidence strongly implicates lipid metabolism, tau binding, immunity, and amyloid processing in late-onset Alzheimer's pathogenesis, highlighting the complexity of the disease beyond amyloid alone.

## Key findings

- Identified five new genome-wide significant risk loci: *IQCK*, *ACE*, *ADAM10*, *ADAMTS1*, and *WWOX* ([paper, Abstract](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-019-0358-2)).
- Fine-mapping confirmed the *HLA-DR15* haplotype as a significant risk factor for late-onset Alzheimer's disease ([paper, Results](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-019-0358-2)).
- Pathway analyses demonstrated significant genetic involvement of the immune response, lipid metabolism, and tau binding proteins, alongside amyloid precursor protein (APP) processing ([paper, Results](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-019-0358-2)).

## Methods in brief

Genome-wide association meta-analysis of 94,437 individuals of European ancestry, comprising 35,274 clinically diagnosed or autopsy-documented late-onset Alzheimer's disease cases and 59,163 controls.

## Trial evidence

This GWAS provides genetic validation for targets actively explored in clinical trials, though definitive large-scale efficacy data proving disease modification via these pathways remains in development. For example, a completed Phase 2 trial of acitretin ([NCT01078168](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01078168)) successfully engaged the *ADAM10* (alpha-secretase) pathway by increasing CSF APPs-α levels. Additionally, vascular risk reduction trials like rrAD ([NCT02913664](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02913664)) and IPAT ([NCT05331144](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05331144)) evaluate interventions targeting the *ACE* locus via angiotensin pathway blockade.

## Public sentiment

Public perception of these genetic discoveries is sharply divided by audience. Science enthusiasts and researchers express optimism about the validation of non-amyloid pathways like lipid processing and immunity. However, the biotech community remains skeptical, noting the recent clinical failures of drugs targeting these alternative mechanisms, particularly anti-tau therapies. Meanwhile, among patients and caregivers, news of 'new Alzheimer's genes' frequently triggers existential anxiety and misunderstanding about the deterministic nature of genetic risk, rather than providing immediate hope for treatments.

## Caveats

- The study population was restricted to individuals of European ancestry, limiting the generalizability of these genetic risk factors to other global populations.
- While the study nominates specific genes at each locus, many genome-wide association loci are intergenic; identifying the exact causal gene or regulatory mechanism requires further functional validation.
- The imputation methods used may have missed rare causal variants that contribute meaningfully to disease risk.
