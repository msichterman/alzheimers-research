**Authors**: Biogen

## Takeaway

The Phase 3 ENGAGE trial demonstrated that aducanumab failed to slow cognitive decline in early Alzheimer's disease. The study was halted prematurely for futility and showed a substantial increase in brain swelling and bleeding (ARIA) among treated participants.

## Key findings

- High-dose aducanumab showed no significant benefit on the primary CDR-SB endpoint compared to placebo (difference of 0.03, p=0.8330) at Week 78 ([NCT02477800](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02477800)).
- Brain swelling (ARIA-E) occurred in 35% (195/558) of high-dose participants versus 3% (16/540) on placebo ([NCT02477800](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02477800)).
- Microhemorrhages (ARIA-H) occurred in 18% (102/558) of high-dose participants versus 6% (34/540) on placebo ([NCT02477800](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02477800)).

## Methods in brief

A Phase 3, multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial enrolling 1,653 adults (aged 50-85) with mild cognitive impairment or mild AD and confirmed amyloid pathology. Participants received monthly intravenous infusions of low-dose aducanumab, high-dose aducanumab, or placebo for 78 weeks ([NCT02477800](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02477800)).

## Trial evidence

ENGAGE constitutes half of the pivotal Phase 3 program for aducanumab alongside its identically designed sister trial, [EMERGE](/trials/aducanumab-emerge-nct02484547). While a controversial post-hoc analysis of EMERGE suggested a high-dose benefit, ENGAGE definitively failed to show efficacy. This unresolvable discrepancy drove immense scientific controversy surrounding the drug's 2021 accelerated FDA approval, leading Biogen to ultimately discontinue the franchise and terminate the confirmatory ENVISION trial in 2024.

## Public sentiment

Public and professional sentiment regarding aducanumab is overwhelmingly negative. Scientists criticized the post-hoc data mining used to justify regulatory approval despite the ENGAGE futility declaration. Caregiver and patient communities expressed profound skepticism, balancing desperation for treatments against the severe risks of ARIA. Today, the drug is widely viewed as a cautionary tale in drug development.

## Caveats

- Because the study was halted prematurely, the final 78-week dataset is truncated, meaning long-term outcomes rely on a smaller subset of participants who completed the trial before termination.
- The severe mismatch in efficacy results between this trial and the identically designed EMERGE trial fundamentally complicates the interpretation of the drug's true clinical effect.
