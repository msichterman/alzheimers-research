## Critical review
**Primary endpoint: met.** The document's designated primary endpoint was safety, and the document reports that MAPT Rx was generally well tolerated with all adverse events mild or moderate, no serious adverse events in MAPT Rx-treated participants, and no deaths, dose-limiting AEs or discontinuations of dosing regimens.
> Overall MAPT Rx treatment was generally well tolerated, with all participants completing the treatment period and over 90% of participants completing the post-treatment period.
**Multiplicity handling:** none stated — the document describes only descriptive summarization of safety, PK, PD, exploratory and clinical endpoints by treatment group and does not address control for multiple comparisons

### Risk of bias

- **sponsor analysis** — The data were held and maintained by the sponsor and analyzed by sponsor personnel; the sponsor also participated in the decision to submit for publication.
- **sponsor design and funding** — The trial was sponsored and designed by Ionis Pharmaceuticals with Biogen collaborators, and funded by Ionis and Biogen; nine authors are Ionis employees/stockholders and three are Biogen employees/stockholders.
- **unblinded safety review by sponsor personnel** — Dose escalations were authorized by a Formal Safety Monitoring Group composed of sponsor personnel after unblinded review of safety data, although the group is described as independent from study conduct and investigators/participants/sponsor remained blinded to group assignment.
- **attrition** — Three of 46 participants (6.5%) voluntarily withdrew during the post-treatment period; exploratory t-tau analyses at 24 weeks post-last dose were reported on reduced numbers (N = 7 for 60 mg, N = 10 for 115 mg) and on N = 5 and N = 3 for the 30 mg and 10 mg groups.
- **protocol amendment / baseline imbalance** — A mid-study protocol amendment changed eligibility, leaving the 60 mg and 115 mg groups with numerically lower baseline CDR Sum of Boxes scores than the other groups.
- **no inferential statistics / sample size not justified statistically** — The document states there was no statistical rationale for the sample size and reports only descriptive summaries; no p-values or confidence intervals are reported.
- **generalizability / selection** — All participants were white and relatively young, with a much higher proportion of early-onset AD than the general AD population.
- **non-uniform follow-up between cohorts** — Cohorts A and B did not transition seamlessly to the LTE and had a variable gap of 5 to 19 months, making their post-treatment biomarker timepoints non-comparable with cohorts C and D.

### Limitations stated by the document

- This first-in-human study of MAPT Rx has several limitations mainly due to its small size ( N = 46) as is typical of phase 1 studies.
- The MAPT Rx doses evaluated in this study achieved the target t-tau reduction of ~50%, but determining whether this reduction is efficacious will require further evaluation in larger, well-controlled trials.
- While there is no statistical rationale for the sample size, it has been selected on the basis of prior experience with generation 2.0 ASOs given by IT bolus injection
- Similarly, MAPT Rx -treatment related effects on exploratory biomarkers will require further evaluation in larger trials.
- Changes in NfL, Nfh, YKL40 and NRGN levels did not appear to be dose responsive and were not concordant, but interpretation of the clinical meaningfulness of these results is limited due to the combination of assay variability and small sample size.
- Lastly, this study was conducted in relatively young participants with mild AD, and it will be important that future studies of MAPT Rx evaluate safety and efficacy in an older population, which may be more representative of late-onset AD.
- All study participants were white, and it will be important in larger, later-phase clinical studies of MAPT Rx to include a diverse patient population to adequately evaluate both efficacy and safety.
- Limitations of evaluating CSF t-tau and p-tau to index target engagement include the clearance of existing tau protein and reliance on tau transport to the CSF.
- While measuring CSF tau protein can index PD activity, it cannot inform on regional differences in MAPT mRNA reduction in CNS tissues.
- Steady-state maximal reduction of the concentration of CSF t-tau was not reached during the 13 week treatment period
- It is unclear why there is no apparent difference in MAPT Rx CSF trough concentration between the 30 mg and 60 mg MAPT Rx groups.
- Comparing the rate of VE observed in our study with that observed in the Alzheimer’s Disease Neuroimaging Initiative is challenging since our patient population was younger (mean age 66 years versus 75 years in the Alzheimer’s Disease Neuroimaging Initiative)
