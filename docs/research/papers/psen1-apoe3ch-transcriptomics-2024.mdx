## Takeaway

Autosomal dominant Alzheimer's disease (PSEN1-E280A) is driven by distinct chaperone and autophagy transcriptomic signatures, contrasting with the microglia-driven profile of sporadic cases. Transcriptomic analysis of a single patient homozygous for the APOE3-Christchurch variant suggests a protective mechanism involving astrocytic LRP1 upregulation and tau uptake. While findings are highly detailed, mechanistic evidence for the protective variant is constrained by the n=1 sample size.

## Key findings

- PSEN1-E280A cases showed significant upregulation of autophagy genes and a higher chaperone-mediated autophagy score compared to sporadic AD (paper, Results).
- Sporadic AD cases exhibited a more pronounced Human Alzheimer Microglia (HAM) transcriptomic profile than PSEN1-E280A cases (paper, Results).
- Astrocytes in the single APOE3-Christchurch homozygote patient demonstrated significant upregulation of LRP1 and lipid metabolism genes (paper, Results).
- Excitatory neurons in the APOE3-Christchurch homozygote patient showed downregulated PSEN1 and upregulated FKBP1B, further suggesting cellular resilience to tau (paper, Results).
- APOE3-Christchurch heterozygotes without delayed disease onset failed to exhibit the protective astrocytic transcriptomic profile seen in the homozygote (paper, Results).

## Methods in brief

Post-mortem observational study using single-nucleus RNA sequencing, spatial transcriptomics, and immunofluorescence. The cohort utilized frontal cortex tissues from 27 donors: 10 PSEN1-E280A carriers, 8 sporadic AD cases, 8 non-AD controls, and 1 PSEN1-E280A/APOE3-Christchurch homozygote.

## Trial evidence

This document reports observational genetic data providing molecular context for the Colombian kindred previously evaluated in the [API ADAD Colombia Phase 2 Trial](/trials/api-adad-colombia-crenezumab), which failed to demonstrate clinical efficacy using crenezumab. A new registered trial, [API-2](/trials/api-2-donanemab-nivegacetor), targets this specific population's APP processing dysfunction using donanemab alongside nivegacetor (a gamma-secretase modulator). Currently, the APOE3-Christchurch findings remain mechanistic and have no direct clinical translation.

## Public sentiment

Public sentiment varies dramatically by audience. General science enthusiasts remain highly hopeful about the APOE3-Christchurch variant, frequently circulating [optimistic discussions on Reddit](https://old.reddit.com/r/science/comments/1dkdsa6/in_an_extended_family_with_an_inherited_form_of/) that wrongly assume broad protection for heterozygotes. Conversely, the scientific community is engaged in [nuanced debates on Alzforum](https://www.alzforum.org/news/research-news/apoe3-christchurch-clings-tightly-tau-averting-tangles) regarding astrocytic versus microglial tau uptake, while maintaining appropriate skepticism over conclusions drawn from a single homozygote sample.

## Caveats

- The APOE3-Christchurch homozygote analysis relies entirely on a single patient (n=1), increasing the risk of interpreting patient-specific biological variations as genotype-wide effects.
- The hypothesis that astrocytic LRP1 upregulation prevents neuronal tau spread is based on correlative static colocalization images rather than functional mechanistic assays.
- Transcriptomic profiles derived from end-stage post-mortem tissues may not capture early or dynamic disease progression accurately.
