## Takeaway

Kisunla (donanemab) is FDA-approved for early symptomatic Alzheimer's disease, showing a statistically significant slowing of clinical decline over 76 weeks. It introduces a finite dosing model where infusions stop once plaques clear, but it carries a severe boxed warning for ARIA, which is especially risky for ApoE ε4 homozygotes.

## Key findings

- Donanemab significantly slowed decline on the iADRS by 2.92 points and CDR-SB by 0.70 points (29%) over 76 weeks compared to placebo (FDA label, Section 14).
- Brain amyloid plaque was robustly cleared, showing a -86.4 Centiloid difference versus placebo at week 76 (FDA label, Table 7).
- Amyloid-Related Imaging Abnormalities (ARIA) occurred in 36% of treated patients versus 14% on placebo (FDA label, Section 5.1).
- ApoE ε4 homozygotes face the highest risk of ARIA at 55%, compared to 36% for heterozygotes and 25% for noncarriers (FDA label, Section 5.1).

## Methods in brief

The prescribing information relies heavily on Study 1 (TRAILBLAZER-ALZ 2, [NCT04437511](/trials/trailblazer-alz-2)), a 76-week double-blind, placebo-controlled Phase 3 trial enrolling 1,736 patients with early Alzheimer's disease and confirmed amyloid pathology.

## Trial evidence

The FDA approval is rooted in the pivotal Phase 3 [TRAILBLAZER-ALZ 2](/trials/trailblazer-alz-2) trial, which successfully replicated efficacy signals from the Phase 2 TRAILBLAZER-ALZ study. A minor reporting discrepancy flagged by the analyst regarding 72 weeks of dosing versus a 76-week endpoint accurately reflects the trial's formal protocol, which included a 4-week observation period before the final primary assessment.

## Public sentiment

Public perception is sharply divided. Retail investors and mainstream media enthusiastically highlight the finite dosing model and top-line efficacy percentages. In contrast, caregiver and neurology communities express skepticism, questioning whether a 0.70-point absolute CDR-SB improvement justifies the intense [logistical burdens and ARIA risks](https://www.reddit.com/r/medicine/comments/what_do_you_think_about_donanemab/).

## Caveats

- The highly promoted finite dosing model is not a permanent fix; plaque accumulation definitively resumes once infusions stop.
- While the relative 29% slowing of decline dominates headlines, patients still experience progressive, absolute cognitive loss during the treatment period.
- Safety monitoring is extremely burdensome, requiring at least five MRI scans in the first year to monitor for potentially fatal brain hemorrhages.
