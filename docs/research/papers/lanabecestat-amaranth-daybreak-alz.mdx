## Takeaway

Lanabecestat, an oral BACE1 inhibitor, effectively reduced cerebrospinal fluid amyloid levels but failed to slow cognitive or functional decline in early and mild Alzheimer's disease. The phase 3 trials, AMARANTH and DAYBREAK-ALZ, were halted early for futility in 2018. The drug was also associated with off-target toxicities, including psychiatric adverse events, weight loss, and hair color changes, cementing the broader failure of the BACE inhibitor class.

## Key findings

- Failed to slow cognitive or functional decline on the ADAS-Cog13 or secondary measures, prompting early termination for futility in both trials (paper, Abstract).
- Significantly reduced CSF Aβ1-40 (by up to 73.3%) and Aβ1-42 (by up to 65.5%), demonstrating clear target engagement despite clinical failure (paper, Biomarkers).
- Increased the incidence of psychiatric adverse events, particularly anxiety and depression, with 33.5% of the high-dose AMARANTH cohort experiencing a psychiatric event vs. 24.5% on placebo (paper, Table 3).
- Caused weight loss of ≥7% in up to 24.9% of treated patients (vs. 12.7% on placebo in AMARANTH), along with notable hair color changes (paper, Table 3).
- Accelerated hippocampal volume loss compared to placebo in the AMARANTH trial (-0.52% for 20 mg vs. placebo) (paper, Biomarkers).

## Methods in brief

AMARANTH (phase 2/3) and DAYBREAK-ALZ (phase 3) were global, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trials evaluating 20 mg and 50 mg daily oral lanabecestat. The trials enrolled 3,940 total patients aged 55 to 85 with amyloid-positive early AD (AMARANTH) or mild AD dementia (DAYBREAK-ALZ). Planned for 104 and 78 weeks respectively, both studies were terminated early in 2018 following an interim futility analysis.

## Trial evidence

This paper reports the terminal readouts for the pivotal Phase 2/3 [AMARANTH](/trials/lanabecestat-amaranth-nct02245737) and Phase 3 [DAYBREAK-ALZ](/trials/lanabecestat-daybreak-alz-nct02783573) trials. The concurrent failure of these trials resulted in the discontinuation of the entire lanabecestat clinical program, including an open-label extension study. The clinical registry data aligns completely with the published findings of target engagement without clinical benefit.

## Public sentiment

Public and scientific perception of lanabecestat is negative but highly inactive, as the drug was written off following its 2018 termination. In the research community, lanabecestat is frequently cited on forums like Alzforum as a textbook example of BACE inhibitor failure, reinforcing the consensus that inhibiting amyloid production in symptomatic patients causes unacceptable off-target toxicities without yielding clinical benefits.

## Caveats

- The AMARANTH trial utilized a strict memory impairment cutoff during screening that resulted in an 'early AD' cohort with a baseline cognitive profile almost identical to the 'mild AD' cohort in DAYBREAK-ALZ.
- Due to early termination for futility, total treatment exposure was significantly shorter than planned, which limited the collection and analysis of post-dose biomarker data, particularly in the DAYBREAK-ALZ cohort.
