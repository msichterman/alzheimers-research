**Source**: [npj Dementia, 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44400-026-00073-z)
**Authors**: Fang et al. — sponsor: Annovis Bio; corresponding author and several co-authors are Annovis Bio employees

## Takeaway

Buntanetap (an oral APP/Tau translation inhibitor) missed both co-primary cognitive endpoints against placebo in the full 351-patient trial. A post-hoc plasma-biomarker check found ~40% of enrollees lacked amyloid pathology; restricted to the 95 biomarker-confirmed mild-AD patients, buntanetap showed a dose-dependent improvement in ADAS-Cog11 versus baseline — not versus placebo, and from an unregistered, non-multiplicity-corrected subgroup carved out of a dataset that had already failed its primary hypothesis. Safety was favorable, with no serious drug-related adverse events. A confirmatory, biomarker-selected Phase 3 ([NCT06709014](/trials/buntanetap-nct06709014)) is underway, with readout not expected before 2027–2028.

## Key findings

- Both co-primary endpoints missed in the ITT population: ADAS-Cog11 LS mean difference 0.13 (95% CI −1.09, 1.35), p=0.833; ADCS-CGIC difference 0.22 (95% CI −0.07, 0.52), p=0.141 (paper, Table 1)
- In the post-hoc biomarker-positive mild AD subgroup (n=95), buntanetap showed a dose-dependent, nominally significant ADAS-Cog11 improvement versus baseline — not a placebo-controlled result, and not prespecified (paper, Fig. 2b)
- Moderate AD biomarker-positive patients (n=115) showed no treatment-related change on ADAS-Cog11; no biomarker-positive subgroup showed benefit on ADCS-CGIC (paper, Fig. 2a, c, d)
- Plasma pTau217/t-Tau testing found only 62.1% (n=216) of enrollees had confirmed AD pathology despite meeting clinical inclusion criteria (paper, Post-hoc analysis section)
- Treatment-emergent adverse events: 33.3% buntanetap (all doses) vs. 26.1% placebo; drug-related TEAEs 8.9% vs. 3.4%; no serious drug-related adverse events (paper, Safety section)

## Methods in brief

Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, dose-ranging Phase 2/3 trial ([NCT05686044](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05686044)); 351 patients with mild-to-moderate AD (MMSE 14-24, ages 55-85) at 54 US sites, randomized 1:1:1:1 to 7.5mg, 15mg, 30mg buntanetap or placebo for 12 weeks. No biomarker or imaging confirmation of amyloid pathology was required for enrollment.

## Trial evidence

NCT05686044 is buntanetap's only completed AD efficacy trial, and it failed its prespecified primary hypothesis. The sponsor's confirmatory Phase 3, [NCT06709014](/trials/buntanetap-nct06709014), enrolls only biomarker-confirmed early-AD patients — directly testing the post-hoc subgroup hypothesis from this paper — with primary completion not expected before 2027-2028. Every buntanetap AD and Parkinson's trial to date is sponsored solely by Annovis Bio, and none has an independently replicated peer-reviewed primary efficacy result. A July 2026 Annovis press release claims NCT06709014 reached full enrollment at roughly 850 patients, above the registry's still-listed estimate of 760 — the registry record hasn't caught up.

## Public sentiment

Public discussion concentrates in investor and trade-press circles; searches found no meaningful discussion in patient or caregiver forums. Patient-facing trade outlets like [Alzheimer's News Today](https://alzheimersnewstoday.com/news/buntanetap-found-effective-safe-early-alzheimers-trial-data/) framed the results as a straightforward efficacy win, while financial press ([FierceBiotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/annovis-shares-plummet-more-40-after-muddled-alzheimers-readout)) has repeatedly flagged Annovis's pattern of emphasizing post-hoc subgroups when primary endpoints fail, across this and two prior buntanetap readouts.

## Caveats

- The headline subgroup finding (n=95) is post-hoc and unregistered, drawn from the same dataset that failed the prespecified primary hypothesis in the full ITT population — a data-dependent selection that 'nominal significance' does not resolve
- The reported subgroup benefit is versus baseline, not versus placebo; a treatment-vs-placebo effect was not demonstrated in this subgroup
- Not adjusted for multiplicity across a subgroup analysis or an 8+ analyte exploratory biomarker panel; GFAP showed no treatment effect and is not emphasized in the discussion
- Corresponding author and several co-authors are Annovis Bio employees (the trial sponsor), yet the paper states 'the authors declare no competing interests'
- Forest-plot (subgroup-consistency) and biomarker-trend claims are described only qualitatively, with no p-values, effect sizes, or figure data available for independent verification
- Raw trial data are withheld until an NDA filing, limiting independent reanalysis

- Exploratory plasma biomarker panel (8+ analytes) reported descriptively without statistical testing or multiplicity correction, then interpreted as mechanistic 'target and pathway engagement' evidence supporting the drug's benefit.
- Small subgroup size (n=95) underlying the study's most favorable claim.

### Cross-examination

11 facts verified against the source document and trial registries. Flagged items:

- **Fact check (unverified)** — Forest plot (Fig. 3) shows a 'consistent favorable effect' across demographic/APOE4 subgroups. Described only qualitatively in text; no point estimates or CIs given, and the figure image isn't available to check. Excluded from key findings, kept as a caveat only.
- **Fact check (unverified)** — Plasma biomarkers (t-Tau, TDP43, IL5, IL6, S100A12, IFN-γ, IGF1R, NFL) trended favorably with 30mg vs. placebo; GFAP showed no effect. Reported descriptively with no p-values or formal statistical testing in the source. Kept out of key findings, noted as exploratory in caveats.
- **Fact check (disputed)** — NCT06709014 enrollment: registry lists 760 (estimated); July 2026 Annovis press release claims ~850 enrolled, reaching full enrollment. Registry has not been updated to reflect the sponsor's later enrollment claim. Flagged in trial_evidence_md and the NCT06709014 trial page rather than silently picking one number.
- **Fact check (unverified)** — NCT06709014 primary endpoints are ADAS-Cog13 and ADCS-iADL. Not stated in the source paper text (which only gives readout timing); sourced from the trials researcher's registry check, marked 'verified: registry' on their end but not independently re-checked by me against ClinicalTrials.gov directly.
- **Registry cross-check** — The document frames the Phase 3 trial (NCT06709014) as building on the biomarker-selection rationale, and the registry confirms this design (MMSE 20-28, amyloid/pTau217-positive required) — consistent, no discrepancy there — but the registry's enrollment figure (760, estimated) is now outdated versus the sponsor's July 2026 press release claiming ~850 patients enrolled; the registry record has not been updated to reflect actual completion of enrollment.
- **Registry cross-check** — The paper's 'no competing interests' disclosure is not something the registry can adjudicate, but the registry independently confirms Annovis Bio Inc. as sole sponsor of every buntanetap AD and PD trial identified (NCT05686044, NCT06709014, NCT04524351, NCT05357989, NCT07284784), reinforcing that the entire buntanetap evidence base to date is single-sponsor-generated.
- **Registry cross-check** — There is roughly a 14-month gap between when NCT05686044 results were posted to the registry (2025-04-29) and the peer-reviewed npj Dementia publication (2026-04), during which the post-hoc biomarker-positive subgroup finding was already being publicized by the sponsor in press releases and conference presentations ahead of independent peer review.
- **Misinformation watch** — Framing the Phase 2/3 trial as a general 'success' or the drug as 'found effective and safe' without clarifying that both co-primary endpoints (ADAS-Cog11, ADCS-CGIC) failed in the full intent-to-treat population and that the positive result is limited to a small, post-hoc, non-prespecified biomarker-positive subgroup.
- **Misinformation watch** — A 2021 company claim (amplified in press coverage) that buntanetap was 'not remotely as good' a comparison implying superiority — i.e., that buntanetap outperforms Biogen's Aduhelm — based on non-head-to-head, cross-trial comparisons.
- **Misinformation watch** — Retail-investor characterizations of the April 2024 Phase 2/3 AD readout as a straightforward positive result; independent analysis (FierceBiotech) concluded the readout was, on balance, a failure once the ADCS-CGIC null result and subgroup selection were accounted for — this discrepancy was material enough to spawn shareholder litigation alleging the company's public statements were misleading.
