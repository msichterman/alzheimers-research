**Authors**: Eisai Inc. and Biogen

## Takeaway

Lecanemab (10 mg/kg biweekly) robustly cleared amyloid plaques and showed consistent clinical benefit, establishing the dosing for its Phase 3 program. The trial also highlighted increased ARIA risks for APOE4 carriers, which prompted mid-study regulatory changes.

## Key findings

- Reduced brain amyloid by 72.5 Centiloids at 18 months, compared to a 1.0 Centiloid increase for placebo ([Registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01767311)).
- Slowed clinical decline on ADCOMS at 12 months (0.077 vs 0.113 for placebo) with a 63.8% posterior probability, missing the prespecified 80% success threshold ([Registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01767311)).
- Showed a dose-dependent increase in amyloid-related imaging abnormalities (ARIA) and infusion reactions ([Registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01767311)).

## Methods in brief

Placebo-controlled, double-blind, Bayesian adaptive randomization Phase 2 trial enrolling 856 participants aged 50-90 with early Alzheimer's disease and confirmed amyloid pathology. The core study tested five lecanemab dosing regimens against placebo over 18 months, followed by an open-label extension up to 60 months. (NCT01767311)

## Trial evidence

This Phase 2 trial ([Study 201](/trials/lecanemab-nct01767311)) provided the critical dose-finding and safety data for lecanemab. While it missed its primary 12-month Bayesian clinical endpoint, the 10 mg/kg biweekly dose demonstrated profound amyloid clearance, advancing to the [Phase 3 Clarity AD](/trials/lecanemab-clarity-ad-nct03887455) trial which ultimately secured FDA approval. The trial also led to a deeper understanding of ARIA risks in APOE4 carriers.

## Public sentiment

Public perception of lecanemab is highly polarized. Patient and caregiver communities frequently express hope and view it as a milestone treatment, though they are often frustrated by Medicare and diagnostic hurdles. Conversely, medical and scientific communities remain skeptical, heavily focused on the serious risks of ARIA (brain swelling and bleeding) and reports of patient deaths.

## Caveats

- The trial failed to meet its prespecified primary statistical threshold for clinical efficacy at 12 months.
- Early safety signals of ARIA-E forced a protocol amendment that truncated the 10 mg/kg biweekly dose for APOE4 carriers, unequally impacting the trial's statistical power for that subgroup.
