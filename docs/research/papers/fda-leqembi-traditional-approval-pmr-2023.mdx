**Authors**: Yasuda & Buracchio — Center for Drug Evaluation and Research, U.S. Food and Drug Administration

## Takeaway

The FDA converted Leqembi to traditional approval based on Study 301 (Clarity AD) showing clinical benefit (letter, page 1). However, due to new safety data showing elevated risks of severe ARIA and intracerebral hemorrhages (>1 cm), the agency mandated strict postmarketing registries and trials to monitor high-risk populations like ApoE ε4 homozygotes (letter, pages 1-4).

## Key findings

- Study 301 verified the clinical benefit of lecanemab, fulfilling the initial Accelerated Approval requirement (letter, page 3).
- Postmarketing Requirement (PMR) 4497-1 mandates a prospective registry (such as ALZ-NET) to track ARIA and hemorrhages in real-world settings (letter, page 2).
- PMR 4497-3 requires a clinical trial to evaluate safety in ApoE ε4 homozygotes, for which the FDA explicitly accepted data from the ongoing [AHEAD 3-45](/trials/ahead-3-45) study (letter, page 4).
- Postmarketing Commitment (PMC) 4497-4 requires analytical validation of an *in vitro* diagnostic device to detect ApoE ε4 alleles before July 2025 (letter, page 5).

## Methods in brief

Regulatory approval and general advice letters outlining postmarketing requirements (PMRs) and commitments (PMCs) scheduled through 2036. The FDA reviewed Phase 3 clinical trial data to verify clinical benefit and assess new safety signals regarding ARIA.

## Trial evidence

The FDA's conversion to traditional approval rests on the Phase 3 [Clarity AD (Study 301)](/trials/clarity-ad-nct03887455) trial, which demonstrated a 27% slowing of clinical decline over 18 months ([NEJM](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2212948)). To fulfill the mandated safety studies for ApoE ε4 homozygotes, the FDA accepted the use of the [AHEAD 3-45](/trials/ahead-3-45) trial. The observational registry requirement points toward umbrella efforts like ALZ-NET.

## Public sentiment

Public and community sentiment is highly mixed. While initially celebrated, the reality of strict monitoring, high out-of-pocket costs, and the risk of severe ARIA has dampened enthusiasm among patients and caregivers ([Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/Alzheimers/comments/1cr0vc2/leqembilecanemab/)). The scientific community is intensely focused on risk management ([Alzforum](https://www.alzforum.org/news/conference-coverage/two-new-deaths-leqembi-highlight-need-better-manage-aria)), while retail investors have expressed frustration over the drug's slower-than-expected commercial rollout due to these logistical bottlenecks.

## Caveats

- The mandate to track rare but severe events like hemorrhages >1 cm and anaphylaxis through 2036 indicates that lecanemab's real-world safety profile is still being actively mapped outside of controlled trials.
- ApoE ε4 homozygotes bear a disproportionately high risk of severe ARIA, complicating the risk-benefit calculation for a significant subset of patients and forcing clinics to navigate complex screening logistics.
