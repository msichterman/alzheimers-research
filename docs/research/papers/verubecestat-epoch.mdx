**Source**: [ClinicalTrials.gov, 2018](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01739348)
**Authors**: Merck Sharp & Dohme LLC

## Takeaway

The BACE inhibitor verubecestat failed to slow cognitive or functional decline in mild-to-moderate Alzheimer's disease. The trial was halted early for futility and revealed paradoxical harms, including accelerated hippocampal volume loss and increased adverse psychiatric and physical events.

## Key findings

- No cognitive benefit: ADAS-Cog difference versus placebo was 0.2 (p=0.6287) for 12 mg and 0.4 (p=0.4625) for 40 mg at 78 weeks (registry data).
- No functional benefit: ADCS-ADL difference versus placebo was 0.5 (p=0.4925) for 12 mg and 0.7 (p=0.3221) for 40 mg at 78 weeks (registry data).
- Accelerated brain atrophy: Total hippocampal volume decreased significantly more in treated groups (-5.6% for 12 mg and -5.7% for 40 mg) compared to placebo (-5.0%) (registry data).
- Slight cortical amyloid reduction: Amyloid load (SUVR) decreased by -0.02 (12 mg) and -0.04 (40 mg) versus 0.00 for placebo (registry data).
- Safety signals: Verubecestat arms showed higher rates of falls (56 and 59 vs 39 in placebo) and suicidal ideation (39 and 45 vs 24 in placebo) out of approximately 700 participants per arm (registry data).

## Methods in brief

Phase 2/3, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial evaluating the BACE inhibitor verubecestat (12 mg or 40 mg daily) vs placebo in 2,211 adults aged 55-85 with mild-to-moderate Alzheimer's disease. The base study planned for 78 weeks, but the trial was terminated early.

## Trial evidence

This document outlines the clinical registry results of the Phase 2/3 EPOCH trial ([NCT01739348](/trials/verubecestat-epoch-nct01739348)). Its termination, along with the subsequent failure of the Phase 3 APECS trial in prodromal patients ([NCT01953601](/research/papers/verubecestat-apecs)), decisively demonstrated that verubecestat lacks clinical efficacy and worsens safety and brain volume outcomes, ending the program.

## Public sentiment

Public and scientific sentiment treats verubecestat as a definitive cautionary tale. While early hope centered on its mechanism of stopping amyloid production, the clinical failure and paradoxical worsening of brain atrophy severely damaged confidence in BACE inhibitors as a viable drug class.

## Caveats

- The trial was terminated early, meaning long-term extension data was cut short and full 78-week completion rates were altered.
- The initial protocol included a 60 mg treatment arm and the first 200 enrolled participants, all of whom were excluded from the final primary efficacy analyses due to protocol modifications.
