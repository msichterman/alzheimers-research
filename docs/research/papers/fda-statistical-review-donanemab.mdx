## Takeaway

Donanemab demonstrates profound amyloid clearance and statistically significant but modest slowing of clinical decline on the iADRS and CDR-SB. However, the FDA's internal review underscores that high trial dropout rates (27% on drug) and missing vital status data make the primary clinical results highly sensitive to missing data assumptions, alongside a potential safety signal of increased mortality risk.

## Key findings

- Reduced amyloid plaque burden by -86.4 centiloids in the overall population at 76 weeks (Table 7).
- Slowed cognitive decline by 2.9 points on the iADRS and -0.7 points on the CDR-SB in the overall population at 76 weeks (Table 4, Table 7).
- Trial dropout rates were significantly higher in the donanemab arm (27%) compared to placebo (20%) (Section 3.2.3).
- Mortality assessment found an estimated risk difference of 1.0% for death at week 76 for donanemab versus placebo (Addendum Section 6).
- Tipping point analyses showed the CDR-SB benefit vanishes if dropouts worsened by just 0.5 points (Table 6).

## Methods in brief

Phase 3 (TRAILBLAZER-ALZ 2, N=1736) and Phase 2 (TRAILBLAZER-ALZ, N=272) multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trials evaluating donanemab over 76 weeks in patients with early symptomatic Alzheimer's disease with confirmed amyloid and tau pathology.

## Trial evidence

The FDA statistical review evaluated BLA 761248 for donanemab based on the Phase 3 [TRAILBLAZER-ALZ 2](/trials/trailblazer-alz-2) and Phase 2 [TRAILBLAZER-ALZ](/trials/trailblazer-alz) trials. While publications frame the efficacy outcomes as robust, this review highlights significant discrepancies. The FDA explicitly advised against using iADRS as the primary endpoint in Phase 3, and tipping point analyses reveal the CDR-SB results are highly fragile due to missing data. Additionally, an elevated risk of mortality (1.0% risk difference) was noted, which is often underemphasized in public trial readouts.

## Public sentiment

Public sentiment is deeply divided. Financial and investor communities enthusiastically focus on market potential and the drug's 'stop-dosing' regimen. In contrast, scientific and medical researchers echo FDA concerns over missing data, dropout rates, and the clinical meaningfulness of small score changes. Patients express a mix of hope and anxiety over strict eligibility and ARIA risks.

## Caveats

- The 27% dropout rate in the donanemab arm severely compromises data integrity; tipping point analyses show the primary clinical benefit vanishes under very mild missing-data assumptions.
- The sponsor proceeded with iADRS as the primary endpoint against explicit FDA advice, introducing regulatory disagreement into the trial's core design.
- The sponsor assumed death was non-informative and treated post-mortem measurements as missing, potentially biasing results and obscuring the 1.0% mortality risk difference.
