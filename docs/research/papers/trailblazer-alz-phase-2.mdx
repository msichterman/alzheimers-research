**Source**: [ClinicalTrials.gov, 2021](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03367403) — NCT03367403
**Authors**: Eli Lilly and Company

## Takeaway

Donanemab achieved dramatic amyloid plaque clearance over 76 weeks and met its primary endpoint for slowing decline on the iADRS (p=0.042). However, standard cognitive endpoints like the CDR-SB and MMSE did not reach statistical significance in this Phase 2 trial. These marginal clinical benefits despite massive biomarker changes prompted early skepticism that was later resolved by Phase 3 data.

## Key findings

- Met the primary endpoint on the iADRS, slowing decline versus placebo (mean difference 3.20, p=0.042) (source document).
- Dramatically reduced brain amyloid plaque deposition compared to placebo (mean difference -85.06 Centiloids, p&lt;0.001) (source document).
- Failed to show statistically significant superiority on the CDR-SB (p=0.139), MMSE (p=0.227), or ADCS-iADL (p=0.230) (source document).

## Methods in brief

Phase 2 double-blind, placebo-controlled trial randomizing 272 adults aged 60-85 with early symptomatic Alzheimer's disease and positive amyloid and tau PET scans. Evaluated donanemab IV every 4 weeks over 76 weeks.

## Trial evidence

This trial ([TRAILBLAZER-ALZ](/trials/trailblazer-alz-nct03367403)) provided the first efficacy signal for donanemab. The discrepancy between the borderline primary iADRS and non-significant secondary CDR-SB outcomes initially fueled debate over clinical meaningfulness. These findings were subsequently validated by the much larger [TRAILBLAZER-ALZ 2](/trials/trailblazer-alz-2) Phase 3 trial, which met all primary and secondary endpoints and led to full FDA approval in 2024.

## Public sentiment

Public perception is highly polarized. Mainstream media and general audiences frequently focus on breakthrough top-line efficacy figures, while patients and caregivers weigh modest benefits against the logistical burden of treatment. Conversely, scientific communities and investigative journalists remain skeptical of the marginal Phase 2 clinical benefits relative to safety risks like brain bleeds (ARIA).

## Caveats

- The combination therapy arm (donanemab + LY3202626) was discontinued mid-trial for futility, causing an unbalanced trial structure.
- Phase 2 clinical benefits were inconsistent across rating scales, relying on the composite iADRS to achieve statistical significance while failing on standard independent measures.
