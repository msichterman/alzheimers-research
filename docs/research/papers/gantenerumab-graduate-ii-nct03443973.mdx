**Source**: [ClinicalTrials.gov, 2024](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03443973)
**Authors**: Hoffmann-La Roche

## Takeaway

In the Phase 3 GRADUATE II trial, gantenerumab successfully reduced amyloid plaques and improved downstream CSF biomarkers compared to placebo. However, it failed to meet its primary endpoint of slowing clinical decline on the CDR-SB, leading Roche to terminate the clinical development program.

## Key findings

- Gantenerumab did not significantly slow clinical decline on the CDR-SB, showing an adjusted mean difference of -0.19 compared to placebo (p=0.2998).
- Amyloid burden was significantly reduced by an adjusted mean difference of -56.46 centiloids (p&lt;0.0001).
- Downstream CSF biomarkers significantly improved, including reductions in pTau-181 (-21.0%), tTau (-17.8%), neurogranin (-14.4%), and NFL (-13.2%).
- Tau PET imaging showed no significant differences in regional tau load between treatment and placebo groups.
- ARIA rates were notably higher with gantenerumab; ARIA-E occurred in 128 treated patients versus 18 on placebo, while ARIA-H occurred in 109 treated patients versus 57 on placebo.

## Methods in brief

Phase 3, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial enrolling 975 patients aged 50-90 with prodromal to mild Alzheimer's disease and confirmed amyloid pathology. Participants received subcutaneous gantenerumab or placebo over a 116-week double-blind treatment period (NCT03443973).

## Trial evidence

GRADUATE II ([NCT03443973](/trials/gantenerumab-nct03443973)) and its parallel sister trial GRADUATE I ([NCT03444870](/trials/gantenerumab-nct03444870)) conclusively demonstrated that gantenerumab's biological efficacy in clearing amyloid did not translate to clinical benefit. The failure followed earlier futility in the SCarlet RoAD and Marguerite RoAD trials, permanently ending gantenerumab's development for early Alzheimer's disease.

## Public sentiment

Public sentiment is highly skeptical following this failure, with patients and caregivers expressing deep frustration over a perceived 'false hope' cycle of amyloid therapies. The disconnect between gantenerumab's biological success (amyloid clearance) and clinical failure has sparked debate in the medical community and fueled public misconceptions that all amyloid-targeting drugs are ineffective.

## Caveats

- The trial was terminated following a pre-planned analysis of safety and efficacy across both the GRADUATE I and II programs, meaning the intended long-term follow-up was not completed for all participants.
- Tau PET results were analyzed at a pooled level with the GRADUATE I (WN29922) sister trial, rather than being specific to just the GRADUATE II cohort.
