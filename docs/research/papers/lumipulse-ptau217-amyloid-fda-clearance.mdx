**Source**: U.S. Food & Drug Administration, 2025
**Authors**: Fujirebio Diagnostics, Inc.

## Takeaway

The FDA granted 510(k) clearance to Fujirebio's Lumipulse G pTau217/Aβ42 plasma ratio assay, marking it as substantially equivalent to existing amyloid pathology tests. This regulatory milestone establishes a pathway for blood-based Alzheimer's screening, though independent clinical validation metrics are not included in this administrative record.

## Key findings

- The FDA issued a 'Substantially Equivalent' (SESE) decision for the device on May 16, 2025 (FDA metadata).
- The assay is classified under Product Code SET: Immunoassay Blood Test For Amyloid Pathology Assessment (FDA metadata).

## Methods in brief

Administrative review of a traditional 510(k) premarket notification (submission K242706) by the FDA.

## Trial evidence

This document contains no primary clinical data. The real-world predictive value of this and similar plasma biomarker assays is being evaluated in ongoing prospective cohort studies, such as the Swedish ADetect trial and the Italian BLAD trial. There is currently no registered trial on ClinicalTrials.gov directly linked to the K242706 submission.

## Public sentiment

Public perception is polarized. The medical and investment communities view this accessible screening tool as a major breakthrough. Conversely, patient and caregiver groups express confusion and anxiety over test interpretation, frequently misinterpreting the presence of a pathological biomarker as a definitive, immediate clinical diagnosis of dementia.

## Caveats

- This document is a regulatory cover sheet; clinicians must look to the forthcoming decision summary or peer-reviewed literature for the test's false positive and false negative rates to evaluate its true diagnostic utility.
