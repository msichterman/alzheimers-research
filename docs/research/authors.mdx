The people whose work this knowledge base summarizes. Extracted from each source document during parsing — name, affiliation, ORCID, and corresponding-author contact — and regenerated by `pnpm publish:docs`, so credit stays attached to the research rather than living in one page's prose.

Contact details appear **only** where the source document published them (a corresponding-author line, an ORCID record). Nothing here is looked up, inferred, or scraped from elsewhere; a blank cell means the paper didn't state it. If you are listed here and want an entry corrected or removed, open an issue on [the repository](https://github.com/msichterman/alzheimers-research/issues).
