## Why it matters

Assesses whether providing physicians with PrecivityAD blood test results significantly alters diagnostic confidence and management strategies for patients evaluated for cognitive impairment.

## Results

Demonstrated that access to the PrecivityAD blood biomarker test significantly impacted clinical evaluation, altering diagnostic decision-making and downstream patient management.
