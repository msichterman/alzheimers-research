## Why it matters

This trial tested whether clearing amyloid plaques with bapineuzumab could slow cognitive and functional decline in APOE4 carriers with established mild-to-moderate Alzheimer's disease.

## Results

Bapineuzumab failed to show significant differences compared to placebo on the primary clinical endpoints (ADAS-cog11 and DAD). While the drug successfully stabilized brain amyloid and reduced CSF phospho-tau, it caused dose-dependent amyloid-related imaging abnormalities (ARIA-E).
