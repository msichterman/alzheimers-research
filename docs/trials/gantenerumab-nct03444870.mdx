## Why it matters

GRADUATE I was the parallel sister trial to GRADUATE II, testing the safety and efficacy of subcutaneous gantenerumab in clearing amyloid and slowing clinical decline in early Alzheimer's disease.

## Results

Like GRADUATE II, the trial failed to meet its primary clinical endpoint of slowing cognitive decline, despite effectively clearing amyloid, prompting the termination of the clinical development program.

## Status log

- **2018-06** — Trial start date.
- **2022-11-14** — Roche announced topline results showing the trial failed its primary endpoint.
