## Why it matters

This Phase 2 trial evaluated the initial safety and efficacy of donanemab, establishing its profound amyloid-clearing capability and providing the first evidence of clinical slowing on the iADRS.

## Results

Donanemab successfully reduced amyloid plaques and slowed decline on the iADRS by 3.2 points at 76 weeks compared to placebo, laying the groundwork for Phase 3.

## Status log

- **2017-12-18** — Trial begins enrollment.
- **2021-05-06** — Primary results published in NEJM showing significant amyloid clearance and modest clinical slowing.
