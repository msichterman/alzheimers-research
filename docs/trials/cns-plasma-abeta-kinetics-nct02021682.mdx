## Why it matters

This study aimed to determine if the amyloid-beta turnover kinetics and concentration changes previously observed in the central nervous system could be accurately detected in blood plasma. It provided foundational evidence validating the accuracy of blood-based Alzheimer's screening using mass spectrometry.

## Results

The study confirmed that plasma Aβ turnover kinetics are significantly faster than in CSF. It demonstrated that the Aβ42/Aβ40 ratio reliably drops in amyloid-positive patients, validating high-precision plasma Aβ ratios as an accurate screening mechanism for CNS amyloidosis.
