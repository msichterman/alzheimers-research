## Why it matters

Designed to definitively confirm the clinical benefit and safety of lecanemab observed in Phase 2b, serving as the pivotal trial for full FDA approval.

## Results

The trial successfully met its primary endpoint, demonstrating a 27% slowing of clinical decline on the CDR-SB at 18 months alongside significant amyloid reduction. These results led to traditional FDA approval in July 2023.

## Status log

- **2019-03-01** — Trial begins.
- **2023-01-05** — Pivotal Phase 3 results published, meeting primary endpoint.
- **2023-01-06** — [FDA grants accelerated approval](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-grants-accelerated-approval-alzheimers-disease-treatment).
