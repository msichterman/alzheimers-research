## Why it matters

This trial tested whether stimulating microglia via the TREM2 receptor could slow Alzheimer's progression. It served as a pivotal clinical test of the neuroinflammation hypothesis supported by massive [genetic studies](/research/papers/wightman-gwas-alzheimers-2021).

## Results

Despite demonstrating sustained target engagement in the central nervous system, AL002 failed to meet its primary endpoint. The drug showed no significant difference from placebo in the change from baseline on the CDR-SB score at week 96.
