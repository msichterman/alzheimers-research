## Why it matters

Tests whether selectively inhibiting soluble TNF-alpha can reduce neuroinflammation and slow cognitive decline, translating [genetic risk associations of the TNF-alpha pathway](/research/papers/eadb-gwas-75-loci) into a targeted therapy.

## Results

The trial completed, but the lack of positive efficacy data, coupled with the early termination of its open-label extension study, leaves the clinical viability of pegipanermin highly uncertain.

## Status log

- **2022-02-01** — Trial started.
- **2024-07-01** — Open-label extension terminated early due to sponsor resource allocation.
- **2025-05-01** — Trial completed.
