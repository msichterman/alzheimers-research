## Why it matters

BioFINDER 2 is a massive longitudinal cohort designed to validate novel blood-based and imaging [biomarkers](/research/biomarkers) for neurodegenerative diseases. It provided the clinical validation data establishing the 96% diagnostic accuracy of plasma P-tau217.

## Results

Ongoing longitudinal data collection. Cross-sectional results published in 2020 demonstrated that plasma P-tau217 could distinguish Alzheimer's from other neurodegenerative diseases with 96% accuracy, performing comparably to more invasive CSF and PET measures.

## Status log

- **2017-05-01** — Trial begins enrollment.
- **2020-07-28** — Baseline cross-sectional data published in [JAMA](/research/papers/palmqvist-ptau217-jama-2020) demonstrating exceptional accuracy of plasma P-tau217.
