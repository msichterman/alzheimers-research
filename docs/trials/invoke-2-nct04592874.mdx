## Why it matters

INVOKE-2 tested whether stimulating microglia through the TREM2 receptor—a target strongly validated by genetic risk studies—could slow disease progression in early Alzheimer's disease. It served as a critical test for translating microglial genetic risk into a therapeutic mechanism.

## Results

The study failed to meet its primary endpoint of slowing disease progression on the CDR-SB. It showed no treatment effects favoring AL002 on any secondary clinical or functional endpoints.

## Status log

- **2021-01** — Trial begins.
- **2024-09** — Trial completes; fails to meet primary and secondary efficacy endpoints.
- **2025-10-29** — [Results posted to registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04592874) confirming lack of efficacy across all endpoints.
