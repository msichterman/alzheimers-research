## Why it matters

Current blood biomarker cutoffs rely mostly on retrospective batch analysis. This trial evaluates whether plasma p-tau217 can accurately and prospectively diagnose Alzheimer's in real-world primary care settings, where patients have greater heterogeneity and more comorbidities.

## Results

Interim data (2024) demonstrated that plasma p-tau217 combined with a brief memory test provides approximately 90% diagnostic accuracy in primary care settings.

## Status log

- **2020-01-01** — Trial begins.
- **2024-07-28** — Interim results published in [JAMA](https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2024.13855) demonstrating ~90% diagnostic accuracy for plasma p-tau217 in primary care.
