## Why it matters

As a sister trial to EVOKE Plus, this study sought to replicate findings on whether oral semaglutide could slow cognitive decline in patients with early symptomatic Alzheimer's disease.

## Results

Like EVOKE Plus, the trial failed to meet its primary endpoint (CDR-SB) at week 104 and was declared a failure by the sponsor.
