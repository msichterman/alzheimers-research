## Why it matters

ENVISION was mandated by the FDA as a confirmatory trial following the highly controversial accelerated approval of aducanumab, intended to definitively prove its clinical benefit.

## Results

The trial was terminated prior to completion due to a strategic realignment by the sponsor to discontinue the aducanumab program entirely, driven by commercial failure and a shift in focus to lecanemab.

## Status log

- **2022-06-01** — Trial begins.
- **2024-08-01** — Trial terminated following Biogen's discontinuation of the aducanumab program.
