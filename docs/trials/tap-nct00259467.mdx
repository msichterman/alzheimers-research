## Why it matters

Evaluates whether personalized psychosocial activities based on a patient's preserved capabilities can reduce dementia-related behaviors and improve caregiver well-being.

## Results

TAP reduced the frequency and severity of dementia-related behaviors and improved caregiver well-being. Caregivers reported the intervention made life easier and improved their ability to provide home care.
