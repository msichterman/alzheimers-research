## Why it matters

This pivotal trial tests whether deep amyloid plaque clearance with donanemab translates to slowed cognitive and functional decline in patients with early symptomatic Alzheimer's, utilizing a unique stop-dosing paradigm when amyloid is cleared.

## Results

Donanemab significantly reduced amyloid plaque and modestly slowed decline on the iADRS and CDR-SB at 76 weeks. However, results were complicated by a 27% dropout rate in the treatment arm and a slight increase in mortality risk.

## Status log

- **2020-06-01** — Trial begins enrollment.
- **2023-08-08** — Primary results published in JAMA showing slowed decline on iADRS and CDR-SB.
- **2024-05-14** — FDA statistical review highlights efficacy alongside significant concerns over missing data and mortality imbalances.
