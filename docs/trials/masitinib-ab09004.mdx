## Why it matters

This trial evaluated whether modulating the neuroimmune system (specifically mast cells and microglia) with masitinib could slow cognitive and functional decline in symptomatic Alzheimer's disease.

## Results

The trial reported a significant cognitive benefit on the ADAS-Cog for the 4.5 mg/kg/day arm, but missed significance for the functional ADCS-ADL endpoint. A higher 6.0 mg/kg/day escalating dose failed to show efficacy. Findings are heavily complicated by a high dropout rate (34.5%) and a late protocol amendment separating the originally designated co-primary endpoints.

## Status log

- **2023-02-28** — Phase 3 trial results published in [Alzheimer's Research & Therapy](/research/papers/masitinib-phase-3-ab09004), reporting significant cognitive benefit at 4.5 mg/kg/day but failing at 6.0 mg/kg/day.
