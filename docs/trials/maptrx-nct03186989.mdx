## Why it matters

This foundational trial tested whether an antisense oligonucleotide designed to reduce tau could be safely administered intrathecally to humans. It established the safety and pharmacokinetic profile needed to advance the drug into larger clinical efficacy studies.

## Results

The drug was well tolerated across multiple ascending doses, with most adverse events being mild or moderate and related to the lumbar puncture procedure. It successfully demonstrated dose-dependent, sustained reductions in CSF total tau and phosphorylated tau in subsequent publications.
