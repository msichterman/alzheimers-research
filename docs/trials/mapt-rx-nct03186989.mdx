## Why it matters

This early-stage trial answers whether an antisense oligonucleotide can safely engage its target and suppress tau production in the human central nervous system. It laid the foundation for advancing [tau-lowering therapies](/research/papers/maptrx-antisense-tau) into larger clinical efficacy studies.

## Results

The trial met its primary safety endpoint with mostly mild-to-moderate adverse events (primarily post-lumbar puncture headaches). It demonstrated strong proof-of-concept by reducing CSF total tau and p-tau181 by over 50% in the highest dose cohorts.

## Status log

- **2017-10-01** — Trial begins enrolling patients.
- **2022-05-01** — Trial completes.
- **2023-04-24** — Results published in Nature Medicine, confirming safety and robust dose-dependent tau reduction.
