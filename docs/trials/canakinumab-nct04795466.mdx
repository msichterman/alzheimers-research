## Why it matters

This trial tested whether inhibiting IL-1β, a downstream effector of the [NLRP3 inflammasome](/research/papers/ising-nlrp3-inflammasome-tau-2019), could slow cognitive decline in early Alzheimer's disease.

## Results

The trial was terminated early by Novartis. Results showed no statistically significant difference in cognitive change between the 10 patients receiving canakinumab and the 16 receiving placebo (LS mean difference in NTB total z-score at Day 171 was 0.069, p=0.6386), failing to validate the therapeutic potential of IL-1β inhibition in this population.
