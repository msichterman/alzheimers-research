## Why it matters

One of two twin pivotal trials designed to confirm the efficacy of aducanumab in slowing cognitive decline.

## Results

Terminated early for futility alongside ENGAGE. Subsequent post-hoc analyses by the sponsor indicated a 22% reduction in clinical decline for the high-dose group.

## Status log

- **2019-08-01** — Trial terminated early following a futility analysis.
- **2022-05-01** — Primary publication of results showing a post-hoc 22% reduction in decline for the high-dose group, [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35542991/)
