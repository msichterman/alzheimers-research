## Why it matters

Designed to establish the safety, tolerability, and biomarker efficacy (amyloid plaque clearance) of multiple aducanumab doses. It provided the foundational evidence of dose-dependent amyloid reduction for the program.

## Results

Demonstrated dose- and time-dependent reduction of amyloid-beta plaques. The study was terminated early without completing the long-term extension due to futility in the Phase 3 EMERGE and ENGAGE trials.

## Status log

- **2012-10-05** — Trial begins enrollment.
- **2019-07-31** — Trial terminated due to futility in the broader Phase 3 program.
