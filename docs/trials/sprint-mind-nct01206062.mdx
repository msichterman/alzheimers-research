## Why it matters

The SPRINT trial was designed to determine if intensive blood pressure control reduces cardiovascular events. The SPRINT MIND sub-study specifically investigated whether this aggressive management also reduces the incidence of probable dementia and mild cognitive impairment.

## Results

The main trial was terminated early after demonstrating significant cardiovascular and mortality benefits. For the cognitive sub-study, intensive blood pressure control did not significantly reduce the primary cognitive outcome of probable dementia, but it did significantly lower the risk of developing mild cognitive impairment (MCI) and a composite of MCI or probable dementia.

## Status log

- **2010-10-01** — Trial begins enrollment.
- **2015-08-20** — Trial stopped early for overwhelming benefit on primary cardiovascular and mortality outcomes.
- **2019-02-12** — Cognitive outcomes published in [JAMA](/research/papers/sprint-mind-blood-pressure-dementia), showing a reduction in MCI but no significant reduction in probable dementia.
