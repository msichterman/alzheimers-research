## Why it matters

SPRINT aims to determine whether targeting a lower systolic blood pressure (&lt;120 mm Hg) than standard guidelines (&lt;140 mm Hg) reduces the risk of cardiovascular events, mortality, and cognitive decline in high-risk older adults.

## Results

The trial was stopped early due to significant benefit. Intensive blood pressure control (&lt;120 mm Hg) resulted in significantly lower rates of fatal and nonfatal major cardiovascular events (HR 0.75) and all-cause mortality (HR 0.73) compared to standard control. However, it did not significantly reduce the incidence of all-cause dementia (HR 0.83, p=0.10) and increased the risk of certain adverse events like hypotension, syncope, and acute kidney injury.
