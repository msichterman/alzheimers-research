## Why it matters

This trial was designed to establish basic safety, tolerability, and biomarker target engagement for etalanetug in a patient population before proceeding to larger efficacy studies.

## Results

Etalanetug demonstrated target engagement with dose-dependent reductions in CSF tau biomarkers (MTBR-tau, p-tau181, p-tau217). Treatment-emergent adverse events were observed across dose groups, including serious adverse events at the 3000 mg and 4500 mg dose levels, in a small cohort of 8 participants.
