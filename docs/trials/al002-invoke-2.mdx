## Why it matters

This trial tests whether stimulating microglia via the TREM2 receptor can slow Alzheimer's disease progression, a therapeutic hypothesis strongly supported by [GWAS genetic discoveries](/research/papers/eadb-gwas-75-loci).

## Results

The trial failed to meet its primary endpoint (change in CDR-SB) and showed no treatment effects favoring AL002 on any secondary clinical or functional endpoints, leading to the termination of the open-label extension program.

## Status log

- **2021-01-01** — Trial started.
- **2024-08-01** — Trial completed; failed to meet primary and secondary efficacy endpoints.
- **2025-01-31** — Open-label extension (INVOKE-2 OLE) terminated due to failure of the parent trial.
