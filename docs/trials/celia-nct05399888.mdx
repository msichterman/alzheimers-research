## Why it matters

Following successful target engagement in Phase 1, the CELIA trial was designed to determine if lowering tau with diranersen translates into a measurable cognitive benefit for patients with early-stage Alzheimer's.

## Results

Public reports from mid-2026 indicate that while the drug achieved robust reductions in brain tau pathology, it failed to meet its primary clinical endpoint of slowing cognitive decline.

## Status log

- **2022-08** — Trial started.
