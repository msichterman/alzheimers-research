## Why it matters

This trial utilized a highly specific AT(N) framework implementation, requiring both amyloid and tau positivity for enrollment. It proved that selecting patients based on precise biological markers can yield significant clinical benefits.

## Results

Donanemab significantly slowed clinical decline at 76 weeks compared to placebo in patients with early symptomatic AD and biomarker-confirmed amyloid and tau pathology.

## Status log

- **2020-06** — Trial begins enrollment.
- **2023-08-08** — [Results published in JAMA](https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2023.13239) demonstrating slowed clinical decline.
