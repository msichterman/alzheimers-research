## Why it matters

The MAPT trial evaluated whether omega-3 supplementation, alone or combined with a multidomain lifestyle intervention, could slow cognitive decline in frail older adults. It provides crucial context on the limitations and challenges of translating epidemiological [risk models](/research/papers/lancet-commission-2020-dementia-prevention) into successful clinical interventions.

## Results

The multidomain intervention (with or without omega-3 fatty acids) failed to significantly slow cognitive decline over 3 years compared to placebo in frail older adults.

## Status log

- **2008-05** — Trial begins.
- **2017-05** — 3-year randomized trial results show a lack of significant effect of the multidomain intervention on cognitive function in frail elderly adults ([PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28359749/)).
