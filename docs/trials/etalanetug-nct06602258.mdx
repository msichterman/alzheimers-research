## Why it matters

This trial investigates whether targeting tau (etalanetug) concurrently with amyloid (lecanemab) provides synergistic biomarker benefits over anti-amyloid therapy alone. It marks a critical industry shift toward multi-target combination therapies for [early Alzheimer's disease](/research).
