## Why it matters

This trial tests whether anti-amyloid therapies can prevent or delay cognitive decline in patients with autosomal dominant (genetic) Alzheimer's disease, a population where amyloid overproduction is the known cause.

## Results

Neither gantenerumab nor solanezumab met the primary cognitive endpoint of slowing clinical decline, despite gantenerumab demonstrating significant reductions in amyloid plaques.

## Status log

- **2021-06-01** — Primary results published showing target engagement but a lack of cognitive benefit for both gantenerumab and solanezumab, [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34155411/)
