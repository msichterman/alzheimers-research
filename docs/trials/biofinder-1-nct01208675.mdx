## Why it matters

BioFINDER-1 is a foundational observational cohort that established the predictive validity of cerebrospinal fluid and [PET biomarkers](/research/biomarkers) for tracking Alzheimer's disease progression. Its longitudinal data directly supports modern biological criteria for preclinical Alzheimer's.

## Results

Data from BioFINDER-1 has been widely published, demonstrating that individuals with normal cognition but abnormal amyloid and tau biomarkers are at high risk for subsequent clinical decline, forming the backbone of major prognostic models in the field.
