## Why it matters

Evaluated solanezumab in mild AD after earlier failures, testing whether targeting soluble amyloid monomers could slow disease progression.

## Results

Failed to meet its primary endpoint; the drug did not significantly slow cognitive decline compared to placebo.

## Status log
