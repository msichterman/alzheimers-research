## Why it matters

Designed to address the high rates of ARIA seen in earlier trials, this study tests whether a slower, modified dose-titration regimen can improve donanemab's safety profile without compromising amyloid clearance.

## Results

A modified titration regimen (350 mg, then 700 mg, then 1,050 mg) reduced the relative risk of ARIA-E by 28% compared to standard dosing at 52 weeks in the restricted population, while achieving comparable amyloid plaque clearance. This regimen was subsequently adopted by European regulators as a required risk minimization measure.
