## Why it matters

To test whether inhibiting amyloid production with a BACE-1 inhibitor could benefit patients who already had established mild-to-moderate dementia.

## Results

Verubecestat failed to reduce cognitive or functional decline compared to placebo, leading to early termination.

## Status log

- **2017-02-14** — Trial terminated early for futility by the independent data monitoring committee.
