## Why it matters

TRONTIER 1 is designed to confirm whether the rapid and profound amyloid clearance seen with trontinemab's 'brain shuttle' delivery system translates into slowed cognitive decline. It aims to establish efficacy and long-term safety for the drug as a next-generation anti-amyloid therapy.
