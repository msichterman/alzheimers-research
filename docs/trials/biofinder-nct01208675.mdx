## Why it matters

BioFINDER is a landmark longitudinal cohort study designed to validate CSF, blood, and imaging biomarkers across the Alzheimer's disease spectrum. Its extensive data collection establishes how biomarkers like p-tau217 change over time and correlate with clinical decline, providing crucial foundations for diagnostic tools and therapeutic endpoints.

## Results

The cohort has yielded extensive validation for blood and CSF biomarkers. Notably, interim analyses demonstrated that plasma p-tau217 increases longitudinally in early Alzheimer's disease, closely correlating with objective cognitive decline and brain atrophy.
