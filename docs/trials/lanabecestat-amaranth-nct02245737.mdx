## Why it matters

AMARANTH was designed to test whether inhibiting the BACE1 enzyme to block amyloid production could slow cognitive decline in early Alzheimer's disease. The trial was halted early when interim analysis showed futility, contributing to the broader abandonment of BACE inhibitors for symptomatic AD.

## Results

Terminated early for futility. While lanabecestat robustly reduced CSF amyloid levels, it failed to separate from placebo on the ADAS-Cog13 or functional measures, and was associated with increased psychiatric adverse events, weight loss, and hippocampal volume loss.
