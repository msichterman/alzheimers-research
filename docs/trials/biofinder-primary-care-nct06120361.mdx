## Why it matters

Assesses whether [blood biomarkers](/research/biomarkers) can accurately diagnose Alzheimer's disease in unspecialized primary care settings, potentially democratizing access to early diagnosis and treatment.

## Results

Interim cross-sectional data from [2025](/research/papers/plasma-ptau217-lumipulse-primary-care-2025) showed an 85% diagnostic accuracy in primary care using a single cutoff. Accuracy dropped to 83% in patients aged 80 and above, highlighting the need for a two-cutoff approach in this demographic that leaves a subset of patients in an intermediate diagnostic zone.
