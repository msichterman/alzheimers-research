## Why it matters

ENGAGE tested whether clearing amyloid plaques with aducanumab could slow cognitive decline in early Alzheimer's disease. Its failure to show clinical benefit, contrasted against conflicting data from its sister trial, sparked major controversy over the [amyloid hypothesis](/research/amyloid-hypothesis).

## Results

The trial was terminated for futility. Final data demonstrated that high-dose aducanumab failed to significantly slow clinical decline on the CDR-SB compared to placebo (difference of 0.03, p=0.8330). Treated participants experienced sharply elevated rates of ARIA-E and ARIA-H.
