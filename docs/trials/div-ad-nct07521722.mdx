## Why it matters

As blood-based [biomarkers](/research/biomarkers) show high sensitivity for ruling out Alzheimer's pathology, they must be validated outside specialized memory clinics. This trial assesses how these biomarkers perform in asymptomatic, diverse populations in standard primary care settings.
