## Why it matters

The API ADAD trial evaluated crenezumab in the world's largest known early-onset Alzheimer's kindred. While evaluating a therapeutic, the underlying observational registry for this kindred has been invaluable for mapping the early longitudinal trajectories of [biomarkers](/research/biomarkers).

## Results

The therapeutic trial failed to show a significant clinical benefit of crenezumab on cognitive decline. However, data from the foundational observational registry proved that plasma p-tau217 elevates up to 20 years before mild cognitive impairment onset in this genetic population.

## Status log

- **2013-12-01** — Trial starts.
- **2020-07-28** — Registry data utilized to map [P-tau217](/research/papers/palmqvist-ptau217-jama-2020) trajectories, showing elevation up to 20 years before symptom onset.
- **2023-08-01** — Trial completed; therapeutic endpoint missed.
