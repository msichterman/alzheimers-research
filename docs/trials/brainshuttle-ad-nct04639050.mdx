## Why it matters

This trial tests whether attaching an anti-amyloid antibody to a transferrin receptor 'shuttle' (trontinemab) allows it to cross the blood-brain barrier more efficiently. It aims to establish if this approach lowers amyloid faster and with less ARIA than standard antibodies.

## Results

No official results are posted in the registry. Interim conference presentations suggest rapid amyloid plaque depletion and improvements in downstream CSF biomarkers with low ARIA incidence.
