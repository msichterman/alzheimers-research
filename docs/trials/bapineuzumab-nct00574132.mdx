## Why it matters

This parallel Phase 3 trial evaluated bapineuzumab specifically in APOE4 noncarriers to determine if anti-amyloid therapy had different safety and efficacy profiles based on genetic risk factors.

## Results

The trial missed its primary clinical endpoints. Retrospective biomarker analysis revealed that over a third (36.1%) of enrolled noncarriers lacked amyloid pathology at baseline, severely confounding the results and invalidating much of the clinical data for this subgroup.

## Status log

- **2007-12-01** — Trial begins enrollment.
- **2012-06-01** — Trial completed.
- **2014-01-23** — Primary results published in NEJM, noting a severe confounding factor with baseline amyloid negativity ([doi:10.1056/NEJMoa1304839](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1304839)).
