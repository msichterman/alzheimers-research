## Why it matters

This trial investigated whether treating metabolic dysfunction and potential neuroinflammation with a GLP-1 receptor agonist could slow cognitive decline in patients with early symptomatic Alzheimer's disease.

## Results

The trial failed to meet its primary endpoint, showing no significant difference from placebo in CDR-SB scores at week 104.
