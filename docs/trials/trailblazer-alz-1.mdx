## Why it matters

Established early proof-of-concept for donanemab, showing significant amyloid plaque clearance and a slowing of cognitive decline that warranted progression to Phase 3.

## Results

Donanemab resulted in a significantly better composite score for cognition and the ability to perform activities of daily living than placebo at 76 weeks.
