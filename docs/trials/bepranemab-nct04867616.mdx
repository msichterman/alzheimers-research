## Why it matters

This trial tested whether clearing tau tangles with bepranemab could slow cognitive decline in early Alzheimer's disease. It adds to the complex [Biomarkers](/research/biomarkers) picture by successfully engaging its target (reducing tau accumulation) without producing a broad clinical benefit.

## Results

No results are currently posted to the official registry. External conference abstracts indicate that while bepranemab successfully slowed tau accumulation by 33-58% versus placebo, the trial did not demonstrate a clear clinical benefit (CDR-SB) in the broad patient cohort. Post-hoc modeling suggested potential benefits for a subgroup of low-tau, APOE4 non-carriers.
