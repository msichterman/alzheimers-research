## Why it matters

Confirmatory test of the biomarker-selection hypothesis raised by the post-hoc subgroup finding in [NCT05686044](/trials/buntanetap-nct05686044) — this time with prespecified, biomarker-confirmed enrollment and adequate statistical power. Its outcome determines whether buntanetap's mild-AD signal replicates.
