## Why it matters

ENGAGE was the identical sister study to EMERGE, designed to confirm the efficacy and safety of aducanumab in slowing early Alzheimer's disease progression.

## Results

Like EMERGE, ENGAGE was terminated early for futility. Unlike EMERGE, the final data analysis of ENGAGE demonstrated no significant clinical benefit for aducanumab on primary or secondary endpoints, leading to profound controversy surrounding the drug's eventual approval.
