## Why it matters

DAYBREAK-ALZ ran alongside AMARANTH to test the efficacy of lanabecestat in a slightly more advanced population (mild AD dementia). It sought to determine if reducing amyloid production could alter the clinical trajectory of patients already experiencing symptomatic dementia.

## Results

Terminated early for futility alongside AMARANTH. The trial demonstrated no significant slowing of cognitive or functional decline compared to placebo, confirming that BACE1 inhibition lacked clinical efficacy in mild AD.

## Status log
