## Why it matters

This trial tested whether blocking the production of amyloid-beta via BACE-1 inhibition could slow clinical decline in mild-to-moderate Alzheimer's disease. Its high-profile failure raised significant questions about the [amyloid hypothesis](/research/amyloid-hypothesis) and the timing of therapeutic intervention.

## Results

The trial found no significant differences in cognitive or functional decline between verubecestat and placebo after 78 weeks. While verubecestat effectively reduced cerebrospinal fluid amyloid-beta, it was associated with higher rates of adverse events, including falls, rash, sleep disturbances, suicidal ideation, and weight loss.

## Status log

- **2017-02-15** — Trial terminated early for futility following an independent data monitoring committee recommendation.
