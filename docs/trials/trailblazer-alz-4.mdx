## Why it matters

Designed to directly compare the plaque-clearing efficacy of donanemab against aducanumab, the first approved drug in the anti-amyloid class.

## Results

Demonstrated superior amyloid plaque clearance for donanemab compared to aducanumab in patients with early symptomatic Alzheimer's disease.
