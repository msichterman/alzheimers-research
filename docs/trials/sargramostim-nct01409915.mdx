## Why it matters

This pilot trial tested whether short-term innate immune system stimulation via sargramostim could safely improve symptoms and biomarkers in established Alzheimer's disease.

## Results

Sargramostim was safe over 3 weeks of treatment. While exploratory MMSE scores improved, the sargramostim group started with significantly worse baseline scores. The standard ADAS-Cog13 assessment worsened compared to placebo at follow-up.

## Status log

- **2011-03-01** — Trial begins.
- **2019-12-01** — Trial completed.
- **2021-03-24** — [Phase II results published](/research/papers/sargramostim-phase-2-ad) showing safety but severely confounded cognitive outcomes.
