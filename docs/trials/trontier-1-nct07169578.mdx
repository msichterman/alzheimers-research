## Why it matters

As a pivotal efficacy study, TRONTIER 1 is designed to confirm whether the rapid amyloid clearance observed in earlier trontinemab trials translates into a significant slowing of cognitive decline.

## Status log

- **2025-09** — Trial begins enrollment based on promising Phase 1b/2a biomarker data.
