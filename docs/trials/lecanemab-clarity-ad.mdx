## Why it matters

Clarity AD serves as the pivotal confirmatory trial for lecanemab, testing whether clearing amyloid protofibrils translates to a measurable slowing of cognitive decline. It established the clinical dataset used for full FDA approval.

## Results

Lecanemab met its primary endpoint, slowing cognitive and functional decline by 27% (-0.45 points on the CDR-SB) over 18 months. It significantly reduced amyloid burden, but increased the risk of Amyloid Related Imaging Abnormalities (ARIA), especially in APOE4 homozygotes.

- **2023-01-05** — Phase 3 primary results published in NEJM showing a 27% slowing of clinical decline.
