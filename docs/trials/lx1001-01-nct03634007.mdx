## Why it matters

This trial tests the hypothesis that delivering a protective APOE variant (APOE2) to the central nervous system can mitigate the detrimental effects of the APOE4 allele. It serves as the closest clinical analogue to preclinical research investigating protective APOE mutations like Christchurch (R136S).

## Results

Interim data indicated detectable APOE2 protein in CSF across cohorts, with reductions in total tau and phosphorylated tau observed in a subset of participants at one year, alongside a favorable safety and tolerability profile.

## Status log

- **2024-08-16** — Interim Phase 1/2 results reported in a review showing target engagement and tau biomarker reductions ([Source](https://doi.org/10.7759/cureus.67037)).
