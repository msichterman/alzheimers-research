## Why it matters

This pivotal trial evaluated lecanemab in early Alzheimer's patients selected using biological criteria. Its success validated the utility of biomarker-driven trial enrollment.

## Results

Lecanemab significantly reduced brain amyloid levels and resulted in moderately less decline on measures of cognition and function than placebo at 18 months.

## Status log

- **2019-03** — Trial begins enrollment.
- **2023-01-05** — [Results published in NEJM](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2212948) showing lecanemab reduced amyloid and slowed cognitive decline.
