## Why it matters

This registry fulfills an FDA postmarketing requirement (PMR 4605-1) for Kisunla's approval. It tracks real-world safety outcomes—particularly ARIA-E, ARIA-H, and large cerebral hemorrhages—outside the highly controlled environment of earlier pivotal trials, aiming to understand risks in diverse populations including ApoE4 homozygotes.

## Status log

- **2024-07-02** — FDA mandates the registry as a condition of Kisunla's approval (PMR 4605-1).
- **2024-10** — Registry begins recruiting participants.
