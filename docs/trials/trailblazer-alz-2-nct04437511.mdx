## Why it matters

This pivotal trial was designed to confirm whether donanemab could successfully clear amyloid plaques and significantly slow clinical decline in patients with early-stage Alzheimer's. Its results formed the basis for the 2024 FDA approval of Kisunla.

## Results

Donanemab successfully met its primary endpoint, slowing clinical decline on the iADRS by 2.92 points compared to placebo at week 76. It also resulted in profound brain amyloid clearance (-86.37 Centiloids). However, ARIA-E occurred in 23.4% of patients receiving the drug, compared to 1.9% on placebo.
