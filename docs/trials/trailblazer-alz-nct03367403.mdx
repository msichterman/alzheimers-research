## Why it matters

This Phase 2 trial tested whether donanemab could effectively clear amyloid plaques and alter clinical disease trajectory, establishing proof-of-concept and laying the groundwork for the pivotal TRAILBLAZER-ALZ 2 study.

## Results

Donanemab slowed cognitive and functional decline on the iADRS score and demonstrated rapid amyloid plaque clearance compared to placebo, establishing robust clinical proof-of-concept for the drug.

## Status log

- **2017-12** — Trial begins enrollment.
