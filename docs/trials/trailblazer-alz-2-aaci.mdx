## Why it matters

This pivotal trial established the efficacy of donanemab in slowing clinical decline in early Alzheimer's disease by profoundly clearing amyloid plaques. It also characterized the substantial risks of [ARIA](/research/amyloid-hypothesis), leading to genotype-restricted regulatory approvals in Europe.

## Results

Donanemab significantly slowed clinical progression at 76 weeks. In the overall population, it slowed iADRS decline by roughly 20% and CDR-SB by 26-29%. However, it caused high rates of ARIA and several fatalities, leading European regulators to restrict its use exclusively to APOE4 heterozygotes and non-carriers.
