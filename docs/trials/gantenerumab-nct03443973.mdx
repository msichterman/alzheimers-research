## Why it matters

GRADUATE II, alongside GRADUATE I, was designed to definitively test whether higher doses of subcutaneous gantenerumab could slow clinical decline in early Alzheimer's disease after earlier trials were halted for futility.

## Results

The trial was terminated after gantenerumab failed to meet its primary clinical endpoint. While it significantly reduced brain amyloid burden and improved downstream fluid biomarkers, it did not demonstrate a statistically significant slowing of clinical decline on the CDR-SB.
