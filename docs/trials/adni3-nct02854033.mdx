## Why it matters

Serves as a foundational observational cohort to track the natural history of Alzheimer's disease, establishing how clinical, imaging, and fluid [biomarkers](/research/biomarkers) change over time.

## Results

ADNI3 validated the temporal sequencing of Alzheimer's biomarkers and supported the accuracy of biofluid markers against PET and autopsy gold standards, heavily informing the 2024 Alzheimer's Association diagnostic criteria.
