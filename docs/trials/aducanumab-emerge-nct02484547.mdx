## Why it matters

EMERGE was identical in design to [ENGAGE](/trials/aducanumab-engage-nct02477800). After being halted for futility, a controversial post-hoc analysis found a high-dose benefit, which served as the primary clinical basis for the drug's 2021 FDA accelerated approval.

## Results

Though halted for futility, a later analysis showed the high-dose group achieved a statistically significant slowing of decline on the CDR-SB (difference of -0.39, p=0.0120), directly conflicting with the sister ENGAGE trial.

## Status log

- **2015-09-01** — Trial begins enrollment.
- **2019-03-21** — Trial halted early for futility alongside ENGAGE.
- **2019-10-22** — Biogen reverses course, claiming a new post-hoc analysis shows significant high-dose benefit.
