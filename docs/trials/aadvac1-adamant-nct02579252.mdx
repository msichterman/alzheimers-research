## Why it matters

This trial sought to establish whether targeting pathological tau via an active vaccine could slow cognitive decline in Alzheimer's disease. While it proved the mechanism is safe and immunogenic, the lack of broad clinical efficacy forced a pivot toward strictly biomarker-stratified testing in future programs.

## Results

The trial met its primary safety endpoint, showing the vaccine was well-tolerated and highly immunogenic. However, it failed to demonstrate significant cognitive or functional improvement in the overall unstratified patient population.
