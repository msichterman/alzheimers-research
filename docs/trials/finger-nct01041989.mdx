## Why it matters

FINGER is a landmark multidomain lifestyle intervention trial designed to assess whether diet, exercise, cognitive training, and vascular risk monitoring can prevent cognitive decline. It serves as a primary piece of interventional evidence evaluated by public health models for mitigating [modifiable risk factors](/research/papers/lancet-commission-2020-dementia-prevention).

## Results

The 2-year multidomain lifestyle intervention significantly improved overall cognitive performance (measured by NTB z-score) compared to standard counseling. Extended follow-up for hard dementia incidence is ongoing.
