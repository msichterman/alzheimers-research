## Why it matters

The ALFA cohort provides critical data on the earliest physiological changes in Alzheimer's disease before cognitive symptoms appear. It serves as a foundational dataset for validating preclinical blood-based markers like [plasma GFAP](/research/papers/plasma-gfap-benedet-2021).

## Results

While no aggregate clinical results have been formally posted to the registry, the cohort has generated extensive peer-reviewed data confirming early preclinical biomarker profiles, including GFAP and p-tau changes prior to cognitive decline.

## Status log

- **2016-10** — Trial begins enrolling participants (registry).
- **2021-10** — Cohort data validates plasma GFAP as an early indicator of amyloid-β pathology in JAMA Neurology.
