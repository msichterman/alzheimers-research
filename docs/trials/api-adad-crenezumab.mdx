## Why it matters

This prevention trial aimed to determine if early amyloid clearance with crenezumab could delay the onset of cognitive decline in individuals genetically determined to develop early-onset Alzheimer's disease. Its failure in the same kindred where the APOE3 Christchurch mutation showed protection highlights the potential importance of targeting tau mechanisms downstream of amyloid.

## Results

The trial failed to meet its primary cognitive endpoint (p=0.43). Crenezumab did not significantly slow cognitive decline or delay the onset of Alzheimer's disease compared to placebo in preclinical PSEN1 E280A mutation carriers, nor did it significantly reduce the accumulation of tau burden in a substudy.
