## Why it matters

This Phase 2b trial established the proof-of-concept for lecanemab's amyloid-clearing mechanism and provided the initial signals of clinical efficacy that justified advancing to the Phase 3 CLARITY AD trial.

## Results

While the study missed its 12-month primary clinical composite endpoint (ADCOMS), it demonstrated robust clearance of amyloid plaque. Extended 18-month analyses indicated a slowing of clinical decline at the highest dose, leading to Phase 3 advancement.

## Status log

- **2021-04-17** — Results published in Alzheimer's Research & Therapy showing significant dose- and time-dependent amyloid plaque reduction.
