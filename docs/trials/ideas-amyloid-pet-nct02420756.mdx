## Why it matters

Evaluates whether access to amyloid PET imaging significantly alters clinical management and health outcomes in diagnostically uncertain cases, providing crucial real-world utility data for [biomarkers](/research/biomarkers).

## Results

Amyloid PET imaging significantly altered patient management in over 60% of cases, demonstrating the clinical utility of Core 1 biomarkers in diagnostically uncertain populations.
