## Why it matters

This trial was designed to definitively test whether clearing amyloid plaques with donanemab safely and effectively slows clinical decline in early Alzheimer's disease. It served as the primary clinical basis for the FDA approval of Kisunla.

## Results

Donanemab significantly slowed clinical decline on the iADRS by 2.92 points and dramatically reduced brain amyloid plaques by 86.37 centiloids compared to placebo. However, the treatment increased the incidence of amyloid-related imaging abnormalities, specifically ARIA-E and ARIA-H.
