## Why it matters

This trial tested whether inhibiting BACE to stop amyloid production could slow cognitive and functional decline in patients who already have mild-to-moderate Alzheimer's disease.

## Results

The trial was terminated early for futility. Verubecestat failed to improve cognitive or functional outcomes and was associated with accelerated hippocampal volume loss, falls, and suicidal ideation.
