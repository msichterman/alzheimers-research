## Why it matters

Tests whether an oral, non-antibody small molecule targeting APP/Tau translation can improve cognition in mild-to-moderate AD — an alternative mechanism to the monoclonal antibodies covered on the [amyloid hypothesis](/research/amyloid-hypothesis) page. Its post-hoc biomarker-positive subgroup finding directly motivated the confirmatory [NCT06709014](/trials/buntanetap-nct06709014).

## Results

Missed both co-primary endpoints (ADAS-Cog11, ADCS-CGIC) in the 351-patient ITT population. A post-hoc subgroup of 95 biomarker-confirmed mild-AD patients showed a nominally significant, dose-dependent ADAS-Cog11 improvement versus baseline; not prespecified or multiplicity-corrected. Well tolerated, with no serious drug-related adverse events. See [Buntanetap in mild-to-moderate AD](/research/papers/buntanetap-phase-2-3-ad).
