## Why it matters

This was the foundational prevention trial in the Colombian PSEN1 E280A kindred. It sought to determine if early intervention with the anti-amyloid antibody crenezumab could delay or prevent clinical Alzheimer's onset in patients with a highly penetrant genetic trajectory.

## Results

The trial failed to meet its primary and secondary endpoints. Crenezumab did not significantly slow cognitive decline or delay the onset of clinical Alzheimer's disease compared to placebo in PSEN1 E280A mutation carriers.

## Status log

- **2026-02-01** — Final clinical results published showing no slowing of cognitive decline ([PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41579901/)).
- **2022-03-01** — Trial completed; failed to demonstrate significant clinical benefit.
