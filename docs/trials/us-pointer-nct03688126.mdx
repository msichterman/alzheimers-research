## Why it matters

Adapting the successful FINGER trial to an American demographic, this study aims to definitively prove whether diet, exercise, and cognitive training can protect memory in at-risk older adults. It establishes the baseline efficacy of non-pharmacological risk reduction independently of core [biomarkers](/research/biomarkers).

## Results

The structured intervention significantly improved global cognitive function over 2 years compared to the self-guided intervention (mean difference 0.029 SD/year, p=0.008). However, it did not significantly alter amyloid or tau biomarker trajectories.
