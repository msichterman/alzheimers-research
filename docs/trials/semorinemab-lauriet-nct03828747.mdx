## Why it matters

Lauriet tested whether targeting the N-terminal domain of tau could slow disease progression in patients who already exhibit mild-to-moderate symptoms, attempting to establish efficacy in later disease stages after early-stage failures.

## Results

The trial reported a 42.2% reduction in cognitive decline on the ADAS-Cog11, but failed to demonstrate any benefit on the ADCS-ADL functional scale, secondary clinical measures, or tau accumulation. The isolated cognitive result is widely viewed as a statistical artifact.

## Status log

- **2018-12-03** — Trial begins screening participants (paper, Results).
- **2023-10-03** — Full trial results published in Neurology, showing conflicting cognitive and functional outcomes.
