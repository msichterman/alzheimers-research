## Why it matters

This trial aims to determine if the rapid amyloid clearance seen in early trontinemab studies translates to clinical cognitive benefit. By using a transferrin receptor 'Brainshuttle' mechanism, Roche hopes to achieve better efficacy and lower ARIA rates than first-generation anti-amyloid therapies.
