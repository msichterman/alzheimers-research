## Why it matters

The first large prevention trial to **enroll by blood biomarker alone** (plasma p-tau217), decentralizing recruitment. Pairs with [AHEAD 3-45](/trials/ahead-3-45) as the key test of pre-symptomatic amyloid removal. See [Biomarkers](/research/biomarkers).
