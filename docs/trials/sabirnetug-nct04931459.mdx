## Why it matters

This first-in-human trial assessed whether sabirnetug, an antibody designed to selectively target toxic [amyloid-beta oligomers](/research/amyloid-hypothesis), is safe, tolerable, and successfully engages its target in the central nervous system.

## Results

Sabirnetug was generally well-tolerated with an overall ARIA-E rate of 10.4%. The drug demonstrated dose-dependent central target engagement of Aβ oligomers in cerebrospinal fluid and produced unexpected reductions in amyloid plaque load on PET imaging, supporting progression to Phase 2.
