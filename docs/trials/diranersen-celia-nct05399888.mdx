## Why it matters

Designed to answer whether the robust tau biomarker reductions achieved by diranersen in Phase 1 translate into a meaningful slowing of cognitive decline on the CDR-SB scale.

## Results

As of mid-2026, media coverage from AAIC indicates the trial failed to meet its primary cognitive dose-response endpoint, though it successfully continued to demonstrate biomarker target engagement. Official registry updates are pending.

## Status log

- **2022-08-01** — Trial begins.
- **2026-07-01** — Phase 2 data presented at AAIC; media reports indicate a miss on the primary cognitive endpoint despite continued target engagement.
