## Why it matters

GRADUATE I evaluated whether subcutaneous gantenerumab could slow cognitive decline in early Alzheimer's disease. Alongside GRADUATE II, it aimed to prove that an optimized, higher dosing strategy could achieve the clinical efficacy that eluded earlier trials.

## Results

The trial failed to meet its primary endpoint. Gantenerumab showed a non-significant treatment difference of -0.31 on the CDR-SB (p=0.0954) compared to placebo. Despite robust target engagement, including a significant reduction in brain amyloid and downstream tau biomarkers, the lack of clinical benefit led to the termination of the drug's development program.
