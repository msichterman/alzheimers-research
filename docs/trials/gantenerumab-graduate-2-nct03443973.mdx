## Why it matters

A parallel twin study to GRADUATE I, aiming to provide robust, replicated evidence of gantenerumab's efficacy in clearing [amyloid](/research/amyloid-hypothesis).

## Results

Failed to meet the primary endpoint, with no significant slowing of clinical decline versus placebo. Only 26.8% of patients reached amyloid-negative status ([paper](/research/papers/gantenerumab-graduate)).

## Status log

- **2022-11-01** — Trial terminated alongside the broader development program after missing the primary endpoint ([registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03443973)).
