## Why it matters

To determine if preventing the formation of new amyloid plaques via BACE-1 inhibition could slow cognitive decline in the prodromal stages of Alzheimer's disease.

## Results

The trial failed to show any clinical benefit. Patients receiving the higher 40-mg dose performed worse on cognitive and functional measures (CDR-SB) compared to placebo, despite successful reduction of amyloid biomarkers.

## Status log

- **2018-02-13** — Trial terminated early for futility based on an interim analysis showing no chance of positive clinical benefit.
