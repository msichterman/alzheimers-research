## Why it matters

This cohort aims to validate new blood biomarkers for biological resilience in symptomatic Alzheimer's patients, building on earlier foundational evidence distinguishing plasma biomarkers across the [disease continuum](/research/papers/plasma-gfap-benedet-2021).
