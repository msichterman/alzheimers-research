## Why it matters

One of two twin pivotal trials designed to confirm the efficacy of aducanumab in slowing cognitive decline.

## Results

Terminated early for futility. Unlike EMERGE, it failed to show any significant treatment effect on cognitive decline even in post-hoc analyses, sparking deep controversy around the drug's subsequent FDA approval.

## Status log

- **2019-08-01** — Trial terminated early following a futility analysis.
- **2022-05-01** — Primary publication of results showing no clinical benefit, [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35542991/)
