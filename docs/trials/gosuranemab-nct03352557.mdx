## Why it matters

This trial aimed to determine if targeting N-terminal tau with the monoclonal antibody gosuranemab could slow cognitive and functional decline in early Alzheimer's disease. It tested multiple doses against a placebo over 78 weeks.

## Results

The trial was terminated for futility after 78 weeks when gosuranemab failed to show clinical benefit on the Clinical Dementia Rating Scale - Sum of Boxes (CDR-SB) compared to placebo. Adverse events were balanced between the treatment and placebo arms, but development of the drug for Alzheimer's was discontinued.
