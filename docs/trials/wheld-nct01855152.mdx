## Why it matters

Evaluates whether training care staff to promote tailored person-centered activities and social interactions improves quality of life and reduces agitation in nursing home residents with dementia.

## Results

The WHELD intervention significantly improved quality of life and reduced agitation and neuropsychiatric symptoms compared to usual care. It also reduced the use of antipsychotic medications in nursing home residents.
