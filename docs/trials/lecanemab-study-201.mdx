## Why it matters

This proof-of-concept trial aimed to determine the optimal dose and initial efficacy signal of lecanemab in clearing amyloid plaques and slowing cognitive decline in early AD.

## Results

The trial missed its primary 12-month ADCOMS endpoint. However, at 18 months, the highest dose demonstrated robust amyloid clearance and a 47% reduction in clinical decline on ADCOMS, setting the stage for the pivotal Phase 3 Clarity AD trial.

## Status log

- **2021-04-01** — Results published demonstrating a 47% slowing of clinical decline at 18 months on ADCOMS for the highest dose, [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33865446/)
