## Why it matters

Validates the high-precision plasma Aβ42/40 assay from [Schindler et al. 2019](/research/papers/plasma-abeta42-40-schindler-2019) in a broad, community-based setting to ensure its diagnostic accuracy translates outside of specialized academic memory clinics.

## Results

Confirmed that a decreased blood plasma Aβ42/40 ratio accurately predicts amyloid PET positivity across a diverse community-based older adult population.
