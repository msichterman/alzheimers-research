## Why it matters

This trial tested whether pharmacological activation of TREM2 with a monoclonal antibody could slow disease progression, acting on the genetic insight that TREM2 loss-of-function increases Alzheimer's risk. It served as the first major clinical test of the TREM2 hypothesis in humans.

## Results

AL002 failed to show clinical improvement over placebo on the primary CDR-SB endpoint, despite demonstrating target engagement in the brain. The drug also caused MRI changes resembling ARIA. The failure led to the termination of the drug's open-label extension study.

## Status log
