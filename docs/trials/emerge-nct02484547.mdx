## Why it matters

EMERGE was designed to evaluate whether clearing amyloid plaques with aducanumab slows clinical and functional decline in early Alzheimer's disease. It served as one of the two pivotal trials for the controversial accelerated FDA approval of aducanumab.

## Results

Despite early termination for futility, a post-hoc analysis of the high-dose group showed statistically significant superiority over placebo on the primary CDR-SB endpoint (difference -0.39, p=0.0120) and secondary endpoints. These results were heavily confounded by the termination and contradicted by the twin ENGAGE trial.
