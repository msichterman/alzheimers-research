## Why it matters

Evaluates whether aerobic and strength exercise can slow cognitive impairment or improve activities of daily living in patients who already have dementia.

## Results

The exercise training program improved physical fitness but did not slow cognitive impairment. Cognitive scores were slightly worse in the exercise group, and there were no meaningful improvements in secondary clinical outcomes or activities of daily living.
