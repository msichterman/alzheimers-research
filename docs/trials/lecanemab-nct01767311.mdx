## Why it matters

This trial was designed to find the optimal dose of lecanemab and establish proof-of-concept for its ability to clear brain amyloid and slow cognitive decline in early Alzheimer's disease. It set the stage for the pivotal Phase 3 program.

## Results

At 12 months, the 10 mg/kg biweekly dose showed a 64% probability of 25% slowing of decline on ADCOMS, missing the 80% primary endpoint threshold. However, prespecified 18-month analyses showed significant brain amyloid reduction and consistent clinical benefit, supporting Phase 3 advancement.
