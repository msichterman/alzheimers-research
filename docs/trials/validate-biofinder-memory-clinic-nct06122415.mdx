## Why it matters

Validates the real-world diagnostic accuracy of fully automated [blood biomarkers](/research/biomarkers) like plasma p-tau217 in specialized secondary care settings, aiming to streamline Alzheimer's diagnosis.

## Results

An interim cross-sectional readout published in [2025](/research/papers/plasma-ptau217-lumipulse-primary-care-2025) showed that the Lumipulse p-tau217 assay achieved 89-91% accuracy for detecting Alzheimer's pathology in secondary care, though older age groups required a two-cutoff approach to maintain optimal predictive values.

## Status log
